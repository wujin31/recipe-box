蒙古牛肉 (Ménggǔ Niúròu) · Mongolian Beef

The Chinese-American takeout classic (which has nothing to do with Mongolia): velveted flank steak fried crisp in cornstarch, tossed in a glossy soy and brown sugar glaze with scallions and dried chilies. Serves 4.

Ingredients
For the beef:
• 450 g flank steak, sliced against the grain 6 mm thick
• 1/4 tsp baking soda
• 1 tbsp water (for the marinade)
• 1 tsp cornstarch (for the marinade)
• 1 tsp neutral oil (for the marinade)
• 40 g cornstarch (for dredging)
• 160 ml neutral oil (for frying)
For the sauce:
• 60 ml soy sauce
• 1 tsp dark soy sauce
• 50 g brown sugar
• 120 ml hot water
• 2 tsp cornstarch mixed with 2 tbsp water
• 1 tsp ginger, minced
• 4 garlic cloves, minced
• 6 dried red chilies
• 5 scallions, cut into 5 cm lengths, whites and greens apart

Steps
1. Toss 450 g flank steak, sliced against the grain 6 mm thick with 1/4 tsp baking soda and 1 tbsp water (for the marinade), then 1 tsp cornstarch (for the marinade) and 1 tsp neutral oil (for the marinade). Marinate 30 minutes.
2. Stir together 60 ml soy sauce, 1 tsp dark soy sauce, 50 g brown sugar and 120 ml hot water until the sugar dissolves.
3. Toss the beef in 40 g cornstarch (for dredging) until every slice is coated, shaking off excess.
4. Heat 160 ml neutral oil (for frying) in a wok over high until just shy of smoking. Spread the beef out in a single layer (in 2 batches if needed) and fry 1 minute per side, until crisp. Transfer to a plate and pour off all but 1 tbsp oil.
5. Over medium heat, fry 1 tsp ginger, minced and 6 dried red chilies for 15 seconds, add 4 garlic cloves, minced and the scallion whites from 5 scallions, cut into 5 cm lengths, whites and greens apart and stir 15 seconds.
6. Pour in the sauce and simmer 2 minutes, then stir in 2 tsp cornstarch mixed with 2 tbsp water and cook until it coats a spoon, about 30 seconds.
7. Return the beef with the scallion greens and toss 30 seconds, just to glaze. Serve right away with rice.

Notes
The baking soda velveting is what makes flank steak tender at this thickness; don't skip the 30 minutes. Fry hot and fast and toss briefly at the end so the coating stays crisp under the glaze. The sauce is meant to be sweet; drop the sugar to 35 g if you prefer it more savory.
