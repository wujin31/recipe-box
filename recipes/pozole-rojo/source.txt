Pozole Rojo — Jalisco-Style Red Pork and Hominy Stew

Pork shoulder and bones simmered with hominy in a broth colored with fried guajillo and ancho chile, served with the full spread of shredded cabbage, radish, onion, oregano, lime and tostadas. Serves 8.

Ingredients
For the pork:
• 1.2 kg boneless pork shoulder, cut into 5 cm chunks
• 700 g pork neck bones or a split trotter
• 3.5 L water
• 1/2 white onion
• 1 head garlic, halved
• 2 bay leaves
• 1 tbsp salt
For the chile sauce:
• 6 guajillo chiles, stemmed and seeded
• 3 ancho chiles, stemmed and seeded
• 2 chiles de árbol (optional)
• 3 garlic cloves
• 1/4 white onion
• 1 tsp Mexican oregano
• 1/2 tsp ground cumin
• 2 tbsp lard or oil
For the stew:
• 1.6 kg canned white hominy, drained and rinsed
To serve:
• 1/2 green cabbage, finely shredded
• 10 radishes, thinly sliced
• 1 white onion, finely chopped
• 2 tbsp Mexican oregano, crumbled
• 4 limes, cut into wedges
• chile piquín or chile de árbol powder, to serve
• 16 tostadas

Steps
1. Put 1.2 kg boneless pork shoulder, cut into 5 cm chunks, 700 g pork neck bones or a split trotter, 3.5 L water, 1/2 white onion, 1 head garlic, halved, 2 bay leaves and 1 tbsp salt in a large pot. Bring to a boil, skim for 10 minutes, then simmer partly covered for 1 hour 30 minutes, until the pork is tender. Remove the onion, garlic and bay.
2. Meanwhile, toast 6 guajillo chiles, stemmed and seeded, 3 ancho chiles, stemmed and seeded and 2 chiles de árbol (optional) in a dry skillet 30 seconds per side, then soak in hot water for 15 minutes.
3. Drain the chiles and blend with 3 garlic cloves, 1/4 white onion, 1 tsp Mexican oregano, 1/2 tsp ground cumin and 360 ml of the pork broth until very smooth. Strain.
4. Heat 2 tbsp lard or oil in a skillet over medium-high and fry the chile sauce for 5 minutes, stirring, until it darkens and thickens. Stir it into the pot.
5. Add 1.6 kg canned white hominy, drained and rinsed and simmer 30 minutes, until the hominy blooms and the broth is deep red. Shred any large chunks of pork with a spoon and season with salt.
6. Ladle into deep bowls. Set out 1/2 green cabbage, finely shredded, 10 radishes, thinly sliced, 1 white onion, finely chopped, 2 tbsp Mexican oregano, crumbled, 4 limes, cut into wedges, chile piquín or chile de árbol powder, to serve and 16 tostadas so everyone garnishes their own.

Notes
Frying the strained chile paste is what gives the broth its round, deep flavor; adding it raw tastes harsh. The bones give body; a trotter or some neck bones make a real difference.

Jalisco-style is often garnished with shredded lettuce instead of cabbage; both are traditional. Pozole is better the next day and freezes well.
