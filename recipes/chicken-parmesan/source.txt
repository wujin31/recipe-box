Chicken Parmesan

The Italian-American red-sauce classic: thin chicken cutlets breaded with Parmigiano and breadcrumbs, shallow-fried until crisp, then topped with a quick marinara and melted mozzarella and broiled until bubbling. Serves 4.

Ingredients
For the marinara:
• 3 tbsp olive oil (for the sauce)
• 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
• 1/4 tsp red chili flakes
• 800 g canned whole peeled tomatoes, crushed by hand
• 1/2 tsp salt (for the sauce)
• 6 basil leaves, plus more to serve
For the chicken:
• 2 large boneless skinless chicken breasts (about 700 g)
• 1 tsp salt
• 1/2 tsp black pepper
• 60 g all-purpose flour
• 2 eggs, beaten
• 100 g dried breadcrumbs or panko
• 30 g Parmigiano-Reggiano, finely grated (for the breading)
• 240 ml neutral oil (for frying)
To finish:
• 225 g low-moisture mozzarella, grated
• 30 g Parmigiano-Reggiano, grated
• 400 g spaghetti, cooked (optional, to serve)

Steps
1. Heat 3 tbsp olive oil (for the sauce) with 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced and 1/4 tsp red chili flakes in a saucepan over medium for 2 minutes, until the garlic is pale gold. Add 800 g canned whole peeled tomatoes, crushed by hand, 1/2 tsp salt (for the sauce) and 6 basil leaves, plus more to serve and simmer 20 minutes, until thickened.
2. Halve 2 large boneless skinless chicken breasts (about 700 g) horizontally into 4 cutlets and pound to an even 1 cm. Season with 1 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp black pepper.
3. Set up 60 g all-purpose flour, 2 eggs, beaten, and 100 g dried breadcrumbs or panko mixed with 30 g Parmigiano-Reggiano, finely grated (for the breading) in three shallow dishes. Coat each cutlet in flour, then egg, then the crumbs, pressing to adhere.
4. Heat 240 ml neutral oil (for frying) in a large skillet over medium-high to 175°C. Fry the cutlets 3 minutes per side, until golden and cooked through. Drain on a rack.
5. Heat the broiler. Arrange the cutlets on a sheet pan, spoon a stripe of sauce down the center of each (leaving the edges crisp), and top with 225 g low-moisture mozzarella, grated and 30 g Parmigiano-Reggiano, grated.
6. Broil 3 minutes, until the cheese is bubbling and browned in spots. Serve with basil, the rest of the sauce, and 400 g spaghetti, cooked (optional, to serve).

Notes
Leaving some crust uncovered by sauce keeps the cutlets crisp. Low-moisture mozzarella browns and doesn't weep like fresh. Leftovers make an excellent chicken parm sandwich on a hero roll.
