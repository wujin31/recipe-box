台式辣椒醬 · Taiwanese Shop-Style Chili Sauce

The cooked, savory red chili sauce served alongside lu rou fan — garlicky, funky with fermented black beans, umami from dried anchovy, and seriously hot. Makes about 1.5 cups (one jar).

Ingredients
• 200 g large red chilis (大紅辣椒), minced
• 150 g bird's-eye chilis (朝天椒), minced — seeds in for max heat
• 80 g garlic, minced
• 40 g shallots (紅蔥頭), minced
• 30 g fermented black beans (豆豉), rinsed and roughly chopped
• 20 g dried anchovies / small dried fish (小魚乾), patted dry
• 250 ml neutral oil
• 2 tbsp soy sauce
• 1 1/2 tbsp sugar
• 1 tbsp rice wine (米酒)

Steps
1. Mince 200 g large red chilis (大紅辣椒), minced, 150 g bird's-eye chilis (朝天椒), minced — seeds in for max heat, 80 g garlic, minced, and 40 g shallots (紅蔥頭), minced. Rinse and roughly chop 30 g fermented black beans (豆豉), rinsed and roughly chopped. Pat 20 g dried anchovies / small dried fish (小魚乾), patted dry fully dry — any surface water will spit in the oil.
2. Heat 250 ml neutral oil in a wok or saucepan over medium-low (~100°C / oil shimmering, not smoking). Add 20 g dried anchovies / small dried fish (小魚乾), patted dry and fry ~15 seconds until fragrant.
3. Add 80 g garlic, minced and 40 g shallots (紅蔥頭), minced. Fry, stirring, until golden and fragrant — don't let the garlic burn or it turns bitter. 3 minutes.
4. Add 200 g large red chilis (大紅辣椒), minced, 150 g bird's-eye chilis (朝天椒), minced — seeds in for max heat, and 30 g fermented black beans (豆豉), rinsed and roughly chopped. Drop the heat to low and fry, stirring often, until the raw chili smell is gone and the oil runs red. This step is the whole sauce — go until the water has cooked out, ~10–12 min.
5. Add 2 tbsp soy sauce and simmer until it tightens to a spoonable paste, ~5 min. Don't substitute oyster sauce here — it fights the flavor.
6. Kill the heat, stir in 1 tbsp rice wine (米酒). Let it cool a few minutes, then stir in 1 1/2 tbsp sugar. Adding the sugar warm-not-hot keeps the sauce bright red instead of dark.
7. Cool completely, transfer to a clean jar, and make sure the oil covers the solids. Fridge.

Notes
Heat dial: deseed the bird's-eye chilis to tame it; push the ratio further toward 朝天椒 to go hotter. Storage: because the water is cooked out and everything sits under oil, this keeps in the fridge for months — a barely-cooked raw chili sauce only lasts 2–3 days. If yours was brighter and more raw-garlic-forward (less funky, no black bean), that was a different sauce — 蒜蓉辣椒醬, a fresher garlic-chili — say the word and I'll build that one instead.
