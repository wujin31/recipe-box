鼎泰豐 辣味黃瓜 — DTF Spicy Chili Oil Cucumber Salad

Serves 4

The iconic DTF cold appetizer. Crunchy, garlicky, sweet-sour, finished with fragrant chili oil. Synthesized from Taiwanese sources (iCook, Pixnet, Angela Diary, 露老爸, 梅子煮藝).

Ingredients
• 4 Persian or Taiwanese mini cucumbers (小黃瓜)
• 1 teaspoon kosher salt (for 殺青)
• 3 garlic cloves, smashed and minced (大蒜)
• 1.5 teaspoons granulated sugar
• 0.5 teaspoons toasted sesame oil (香油)
• 1 teaspoon chili oil (辣油) — DTF brand, homemade 油潑辣子, or Lao Gan Ma
• 1 tablespoon sushi vinegar 壽司醋 (or white rice vinegar)
• 0.5 red chili pepper, seeded and sliced into strips (辣椒絲)

Steps
1. Scrub & cut
    Scrub 4 Persian or Taiwanese mini cucumbers (小黃瓜) under running water with a vegetable brush — you're eating these raw. Cut into rounds about 1.5–2 cm thick.
2. Smash (拍碎)
    Use the flat side of your knife to gently press each round — just enough to crack it without fully breaking it. The jagged edges hold the dressing better than clean cuts.
3. Salt & draw out bitter water (殺青)
    15m
    Toss cucumbers with 1 teaspoon kosher salt (for 殺青) in a bowl or container. Let sit 10–20 minutes. Bitter green water pools at the bottom — that's what you want out. Taste after 10 min; don't let it go too long or they'll get too salty.
4. Rinse & drain
    Pour off the bitter water, rinse thoroughly with cold filtered or boiled-then-cooled water (since they're eaten raw). Drain well and pat dry.
5. Combine (搖搖樂)
    Transfer cucumbers to a lidded container. Add 3 garlic cloves, smashed and minced (大蒜), 1.5 teaspoons granulated sugar, 0.5 teaspoons toasted sesame oil (香油), 1 tablespoon sushi vinegar 壽司醋 (or white rice vinegar), and 0.5 red chili pepper, seeded and sliced into strips (辣椒絲). Seal and shake to coat everything evenly.
6. Chill & marinate
    1h
    Refrigerate at least 30–60 minutes, shaking or turning the container a few times. For best results, go 2–3 hours or overnight — flavor deepens significantly.
7. Finish with chili oil & serve
    Just before plating, drizzle 1 teaspoon chili oil (辣油) — DTF brand, homemade 油潑辣子, or Lao Gan Ma over the top — don't mix it in during marinating or it loses its fragrance. Garnish with the marinated garlic and chili strips. Serve ice cold.

Notes
Secret weapons: A pinch of ground 花椒, a few drops of soy sauce, and/or MSG all bring it closer to DTF's actual flavor. Double vinegar (烏醋 + 白醋) adds depth. Granulated sugar subbed for rock sugar here — slightly reduced to 1.5 tsp since it's sweeter by volume. The 殺青 salting step is non-negotiable for crunch. Rinse with clean water since these are eaten raw. Keeps 2–3 days in the fridge; crunch peaks day 1.
