Loco Moco — Hawaiian Hamburger, Egg and Gravy Rice Bowl

Hilo's 1949 invention: a juicy seasoned beef patty on rice, smothered in onion-mushroom brown gravy made in the same pan and crowned with a sunny-side-up egg. Serves 4.

Ingredients
For the patties:
• 680 g ground beef (80/20)
• 30 g panko
• 1 egg
• 1/2 onion, grated
• 1 tbsp shoyu (soy sauce)
• 3/4 tsp salt
• 1/2 tsp black pepper
• 1 tbsp neutral oil
For the gravy:
• 2 tbsp butter
• 1/2 onion, thinly sliced (for the gravy)
• 150 g button mushrooms, sliced
• 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
• 480 ml beef stock
• 1 tbsp shoyu (for the gravy)
• 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
To serve:
• 4 eggs
• 900 g hot cooked white rice
• 2 scallions, sliced

Steps
1. Mix 680 g ground beef (80/20), 30 g panko, 1 egg, 1/2 onion, grated, 1 tbsp shoyu (soy sauce), 3/4 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp black pepper just until combined. Shape into 4 patties about 2 cm thick, with a dimple in the center so they stay flat.
2. Heat 1 tbsp neutral oil in a large skillet over medium-high and cook the patties 4 minutes per side, until browned and cooked through. Transfer to a plate and pour off all but 1 tbsp fat.
3. Add 2 tbsp butter to the pan with 1/2 onion, thinly sliced (for the gravy) and 150 g button mushrooms, sliced. Cook 5 minutes over medium, scraping up the browned bits, until the mushrooms are golden.
4. Sprinkle in 2 tbsp all-purpose flour and stir 1 minute. Whisk in 480 ml beef stock, 1 tbsp shoyu (for the gravy) and 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce, bring to a simmer and cook 3 minutes, until thick enough to coat a spoon. Return the patties to the gravy to warm through.
5. In a separate pan, fry 4 eggs sunny side up, about 3 minutes, until the whites are set and the yolks runny.
6. Scoop 900 g hot cooked white rice into bowls, top each with a patty, plenty of gravy and an egg. Scatter with 2 scallions, sliced.

Notes
The gravy is the point: make it in the patty pan so it picks up the fond. Add a splash more stock if it tightens while standing. Shoyu in both the patty and the gravy is the local touch. Some spots add Spam or Portuguese sausage alongside.
