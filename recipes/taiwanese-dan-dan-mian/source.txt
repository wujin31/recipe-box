台式擔擔麵 (Taiwanese Dan Dan Mian) — all-sesame, celery version

Taiwanese-style dry dan dan noodles with Lao Gan Ma chili crisp and an all-sesame sauce. Celery stands in for preserved mustard tuber (榨菜). No peanut butter — sesame paste carries the body, leaning this closer to a pure Sichuan profile.

Ingredients
• 200 g 關廟麵 or dried thin noodles (陽春麵)
• 60 g bok choy or baby spinach (青菜)
• 4 tbsp white sesame paste (白芝麻醬)
• 2 tbsp soy sauce (醬油)
• 1 tbsp black vinegar / Zhenjiang vinegar (烏醋 or 鎮江醋)
• 3/4 tsp sugar (砂糖)
• 2 tbsp garlic, minced to paste (蒜泥) — added raw
• 1 tsp sesame oil (香油)
• 1 1/2 tbsp Lao Gan Ma chili crisp (老干媽辣椒醬) — spoon from bottom of jar
• 1/2 tsp Sichuan pepper oil (花椒油) — optional but recommended
• 3 tbsp cold drinking water (to thin sauce)
• 150 g ground pork (豬絞肉)
• 50 g celery, finely diced (西芹) — stands in for 榨菜
• 1 tbsp Lao Gan Ma chili crisp (for pork topping)
• 1 tbsp soy sauce (for pork topping)
• 1 tbsp Shaoxing rice wine (紹興酒)
• 1/2 tsp sugar (for pork topping)
• 1/4 tsp salt (for celery, to taste)
• 1 tbsp neutral oil
• 3 tbsp scallions, thinly sliced (蔥花)
• 2 tbsp crushed roasted peanuts (花生碎) — optional garnish, omit if avoiding peanuts

Steps
1. Preheat the stainless pan over medium-high 2–3 min; water-drop test should bead and skate. Add 1 tbsp neutral oil, let it shimmer, then 50 g celery, finely diced (西芹) — stands in for 榨菜 and a pinch of 1/4 tsp salt (for celery, to taste). Stir-fry 1 min. Add 150 g ground pork (豬絞肉), spread it out, and let it sear undisturbed 30–60 sec so it self-releases before you break it apart. Once no longer pink, add 1 tbsp Lao Gan Ma chili crisp (for pork topping), 1 tbsp soy sauce (for pork topping), 1 tbsp Shaoxing rice wine (紹興酒) (scrape up the fond), and 1/2 tsp sugar (for pork topping). Stir-fry until cooked through and dry. Taste and adjust salt. Set aside.
2. Put 4 tbsp white sesame paste (白芝麻醬) in a bowl. Add 2 tbsp soy sauce (醬油), 1 tbsp black vinegar / Zhenjiang vinegar (烏醋 or 鎮江醋), 3/4 tsp sugar (砂糖), 2 tbsp garlic, minced to paste (蒜泥) — added raw, 1 tsp sesame oil (香油), 1 1/2 tbsp Lao Gan Ma chili crisp (老干媽辣椒醬) — spoon from bottom of jar, and 1/2 tsp Sichuan pepper oil (花椒油) — optional but recommended. Whisk in 3 tbsp cold drinking water (to thin sauce) gradually — the paste will seize and clump first, then loosen as you add water. Keep going until smooth and pourable but still thick. Taste: nutty-toasty first, tangy-spicy finish, faint sweetness. Adjust vinegar/chili to preference.
3. Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Loosen 200 g 關廟麵 or dried thin noodles (陽春麵) before dropping in. Cook ~1 min (or per package). In the last 30 sec, drop 60 g bok choy or baby spinach (青菜) into the same water — 30–45 sec, halve baby bok choy lengthwise first so stems cook through. Drain noodles and greens, then immediately rinse noodles with cold water to keep them springy (Q彈). Shake off excess water well — wet greens dilute the sauce.
4. Pour the sauce into the bottom of each bowl. Add hot drained noodles, then greens. Spoon pork topping over. Finish with 3 tbsp scallions, thinly sliced (蔥花) (and 2 tbsp crushed roasted peanuts (花生碎) — optional garnish, omit if avoiding peanuts or a sesame-seed / fried-shallot swap). Toss immediately — the sauce sits at the bottom and dries out fast. If it's tight, loosen with a splash of hot noodle water. Eat hot.

Notes
No peanut butter: Sesame paste is bumped from 3 to 4 tbsp to carry the body the peanut used to add. Flavor leans more toward pure Sichuan (all-sesame) than the nuttier Taiwanese style — still excellent, just a touch more bitter-toasty and less rounded. A little extra sugar (¾ tsp) rebalances it.

- If you're avoiding peanuts entirely, also skip the crushed peanut garnish (0020) — swap in toasted white sesame seeds or fried shallots (油蔥酥) for crunch.
- Sesame paste seizes before it loosens — whisk the water in slowly, a little at a time. If it clumps at assembly, a splash of hot noodle water smooths it out.

Celery swap: pure crunch, no salt or ferment. Salt while frying and lean on the soy. When you find 榨菜 (or 菜脯), swap 1:1 and drop the added salt.

Stainless pan: preheat 2–3 min, water-drop test (beads skate, don't sizzle), then oil. Let pork sear undisturbed 30–60 sec so it self-releases before stirring. The Shaoxing wine deglazes the fond — scrape it back in. Don't let the pan run dry before the wine.

Lao Gan Ma: spoon from the bottom for oil + crispy bits. Garlic goes in raw — sauce keeps a week without garlic, add fresh each time. Served dry (乾拌); for soup style add a ladle of broth first.
