LA Street Dogs — Bacon-Wrapped Hot Dogs with Grilled Onions and Peppers

The Los Angeles "danger dog" from the carts outside concerts and ballgames: bacon-wrapped franks griddled crisp, piled with onions and peppers cooked in the bacon fat, mayo, ketchup, mustard and a charred whole jalapeño. Serves 4 (8 dogs).

Ingredients
• 8 all-beef hot dogs
• 8 slices thin-cut bacon
• 8 soft hot dog buns
• 1 large white onion, sliced
• 1 green bell pepper, sliced
• 8 jalapeños
• 1/4 tsp salt
• 4 tbsp mayonnaise
• 4 tbsp ketchup
• 4 tbsp yellow mustard
• 1 tomato, finely diced
• 1/4 white onion, finely diced
• 2 tbsp chopped cilantro

Steps
1. Wrap each of 8 all-beef hot dogs in a slice of 8 slices thin-cut bacon in a tight spiral, tucking or pinning the ends with toothpicks.
2. Heat a griddle or large cast-iron pan over medium. Cook the dogs seam side down first, turning, for 10 minutes, until the bacon is crisp all over.
3. Push the dogs to the side and cook 1 large white onion, sliced, 1 green bell pepper, sliced and 1/4 tsp salt in the rendered bacon fat for 10 minutes, stirring, until soft and charred in spots. At the same time, char 8 jalapeños whole for 8 minutes, turning.
4. Warm 8 soft hot dog buns on the griddle for 1 minute. Mix 1 tomato, finely diced, 1/4 white onion, finely diced and 2 tbsp chopped cilantro for a quick pico.
5. Spread the buns with 4 tbsp mayonnaise, add a dog, pile on onions and peppers, then zigzag 4 tbsp ketchup and 4 tbsp yellow mustard on top and spoon on the pico. Serve each with a charred jalapeño on the side.

Notes
Thin bacon crisps in the same time it takes the hot dog to heat through; thick bacon stays flabby. Remove the toothpicks before serving. Bolillo rolls are the other popular bun.
