Sheet-Pan Bibimbap

A weeknight bibimbap: marinated beef and the vegetables roasted on one sheet pan instead of stir-fried one by one, piled on rice with fried eggs and a sesame gochujang sauce. Serves 4.

Ingredients
For the beef:
• 450 g thinly sliced ribeye or sirloin (bulgogi cut)
• 2 tbsp soy sauce
• 1 tbsp brown sugar
• 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
• 2 garlic cloves, grated
For the vegetables:
• 2 carrots, cut into matchsticks
• 1 zucchini, cut into half-moons
• 200 g shiitake mushrooms, sliced
• 3 tbsp neutral oil
• 1/2 tsp salt
• 200 g baby spinach
• 1 tsp toasted sesame oil (for the spinach)
For the sauce:
• 3 tbsp gochujang
• 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil (for the sauce)
• 1 tbsp rice vinegar
• 1 tbsp honey
• 1 tbsp water
To serve:
• 900 g hot cooked short-grain rice
• 4 eggs
• 200 g kimchi
• 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds

Steps
1. Heat the oven to 230°C. Toss 450 g thinly sliced ribeye or sirloin (bulgogi cut) with 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp brown sugar, 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil and 2 garlic cloves, grated and marinate while you prep.
2. Toss 2 carrots, cut into matchsticks, 1 zucchini, cut into half-moons and 200 g shiitake mushrooms, sliced separately with 3 tbsp neutral oil and 1/2 tsp salt, and spread them in their own sections on a large sheet pan. Roast 12 minutes.
3. Push the vegetables together and spread the beef on the free part of the pan in a thin layer. Roast 6 minutes more, until the beef is cooked and the vegetables are browned at the edges.
4. Meanwhile, wilt 200 g baby spinach in a hot pan for 1 minute and toss with 1 tsp toasted sesame oil (for the spinach) and a pinch of salt. Whisk 3 tbsp gochujang, 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil (for the sauce), 1 tbsp rice vinegar, 1 tbsp honey and 1 tbsp water.
5. Fry 4 eggs sunny side up for 3 minutes, until the whites are crisp at the edges.
6. Divide 900 g hot cooked short-grain rice among bowls and arrange the beef, vegetables, spinach and 200 g kimchi on top in sections. Top with an egg, sauce and 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds. Mix everything together before eating.

Notes
For dolsot-style crispy rice, press the rice into a hot oiled cast-iron pan and cook undisturbed for 8 minutes before topping. Bean sprouts (blanched 2 minutes, dressed with sesame oil and salt) are a classic addition.
