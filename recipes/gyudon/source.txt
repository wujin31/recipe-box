牛丼 (Gyūdon) · Japanese Beef Bowl

The Yoshinoya-style bowl: paper-thin beef and onion simmered in a sweet dashi-soy broth and ladled over rice with plenty of sauce, topped with red ginger and an egg yolk. Serves 2.

Ingredients
• 340 g thinly sliced beef ribeye or chuck (shabu-shabu cut)
• 1 onion, sliced 5 mm thick
• 120 ml dashi
• 1 tbsp sake
• 1 tbsp sugar
• 2 tbsp mirin
• 2 tbsp soy sauce
• 1 tsp grated ginger
• 450 g hot cooked short-grain rice
• 2 egg yolks or onsen tamago (optional)
• 2 tbsp beni shoga (red pickled ginger)
• 1 scallion, thinly sliced
• shichimi togarashi, to serve

Steps
1. Put 120 ml dashi, 1 tbsp sake, 1 tbsp sugar, 2 tbsp mirin and 2 tbsp soy sauce in a skillet, cover and bring to a boil.
2. Add 1 onion, sliced 5 mm thick in an even layer, cover and simmer on medium for 5 minutes, until soft and translucent.
3. Spread 340 g thinly sliced beef ribeye or chuck (shabu-shabu cut) over the onion, separating the slices, and add 1 tsp grated ginger. Simmer 2 minutes, until no longer pink, skimming the scum and fat from the surface.
4. Turn off the heat and let it sit 2 minutes so the beef absorbs the broth.
5. Divide 450 g hot cooked short-grain rice between bowls, top with the beef and onion and spoon over generous sauce ("tsuyudaku" style). Add 2 egg yolks or onsen tamago (optional), 2 tbsp beni shoga (red pickled ginger), 1 scallion, thinly sliced and shichimi togarashi, to serve.

Notes
Partially frozen beef slices paper-thin with a sharp knife; or buy shabu-shabu beef at a Japanese or Korean market. Chuck with good marbling gives the closest chain-restaurant flavor. Don't boil the beef long; it toughens quickly.
