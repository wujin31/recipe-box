親子丼 (Oyakodon) · Chicken and Egg Rice Bowl

"Parent and child" bowl: chicken thigh and onion simmered in dashi, soy and mirin, then set with softly beaten egg and slid over rice. Serves 2.

Ingredients
• 300 g boneless chicken thighs, cut into bite-size pieces
• 1/2 onion, thinly sliced
• 160 ml dashi
• 2 tbsp soy sauce
• 2 tbsp mirin
• 2 tbsp sake
• 2 tsp sugar
• 4 eggs
• 450 g hot cooked short-grain rice
• a few sprigs mitsuba or 1 scallion, chopped
• shichimi togarashi, to serve

Steps
1. Put 160 ml dashi, 2 tbsp soy sauce, 2 tbsp mirin, 2 tbsp sake and 2 tsp sugar in a small skillet (an oyakodon pan or 20 cm frying pan) and bring to a simmer.
2. Add 1/2 onion, thinly sliced and simmer 3 minutes, until softened. Add 300 g boneless chicken thighs, cut into bite-size pieces in a single layer and simmer 5 minutes, turning once, until cooked through. Skim any foam.
3. Lightly beat 4 eggs with chopsticks, just 10 strokes, so streaks of white and yolk remain.
4. Pour two-thirds of the egg over the chicken in a circle, cover and cook on medium-low for 30 seconds. Pour in the rest, add a few sprigs mitsuba or 1 scallion, chopped, cover and turn off the heat. Leave 30 seconds, until the egg is just set but still glossy.
5. Divide 450 g hot cooked short-grain rice between deep bowls and slide half of the chicken and egg over each, with its sauce. Serve with shichimi togarashi, to serve.

Notes
For individual bowls, cook in two batches with half the sauce each so the egg can be slid neatly onto the rice. The two-stage egg pour gives both set and runny egg, the hallmark of a good oyakodon. Use pasteurized eggs if you're worried about the soft finish.
