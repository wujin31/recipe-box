Crispy Gnocchi with Burst Tomatoes and Mozzarella

Pan-seared crispy gnocchi folded into cherry tomatoes burst in garlicky butter, topped with fresh mozzarella and basil and broiled until bubbling and browned in spots. 25 minutes. Serves 3.

Ingredients
• 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
• 500 g shelf-stable or refrigerated potato gnocchi
• 2 tbsp unsalted butter
• 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
• 1/2 tsp red chili flakes
• 1/2 tsp salt
• 1/4 tsp black pepper
• 450 g cherry or grape tomatoes
• 60 ml water
• 15 g basil leaves
• 225 g fresh mozzarella, torn into pieces

Steps
1. Heat the broiler with a rack 15 cm from the heat.
2. Heat 2 tbsp of 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil in a large ovenproof skillet over medium-high. Add 500 g shelf-stable or refrigerated potato gnocchi in one layer, cover, and cook 4 minutes, until golden underneath. Stir and cook 2 minutes more. Transfer to a plate.
3. Lower the heat to medium, add 2 tbsp unsalted butter and cook 1 minute, until golden-brown. Add 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced, 1/2 tsp red chili flakes, 1/2 tsp salt and 1/4 tsp black pepper and stir 30 seconds.
4. Add 450 g cherry or grape tomatoes and 60 ml water, cover and cook 5 minutes, until the tomatoes soften and start to burst. Press them with a spoon to release their juices.
5. Stir in the gnocchi and most of 15 g basil leaves. Top with 225 g fresh mozzarella, torn into pieces and drizzle with the remaining oil.
6. Broil 3 minutes, until the cheese is melted and browned in spots. Scatter with the rest of the basil and serve from the pan.

Notes
Inspired by Ali Slagle's NYT Cooking recipe. Use an ovenproof skillet (cast iron is ideal). Pat the mozzarella dry so it browns rather than weeps.
