Lemon Chicken Pasta

Golden seared chicken thighs with spaghetti in a bright al limone-style sauce of lemon zest and juice, butter, Parmigiano and starchy pasta water, finished with parsley and black pepper. Serves 4.

Ingredients
• 600 g boneless skinless chicken thighs
• 3/4 tsp salt (for the chicken)
• 1 tsp black pepper
• 1 tbsp olive oil
• 400 g spaghetti
• 1 tbsp salt (for the pasta water)
• 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
• 4 tbsp butter
• 2 lemons, zested and juiced (about 80 ml juice)
• 60 g Parmigiano-Reggiano, finely grated
• 10 g parsley, chopped
• 1/4 tsp red chili flakes

Steps
1. Season 600 g boneless skinless chicken thighs with 3/4 tsp salt (for the chicken) and half of 1 tsp black pepper. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high and cook 5 minutes per side, until golden and 74°C inside. Rest on a board, then slice.
2. Cook 400 g spaghetti in boiling water with 1 tbsp salt (for the pasta water) 1 minute less than the package says. Reserve 300 ml pasta water.
3. Lower the heat under the skillet to medium. Add 1 tbsp of 4 tbsp butter and 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced and cook 1 minute, until pale gold. Add 1/4 tsp red chili flakes, the zest of 2 lemons, zested and juiced (about 80 ml juice), half the lemon juice and 180 ml pasta water, scraping up the browned bits.
4. Add the pasta and the remaining butter and toss over medium heat for 2 minutes, until the sauce turns creamy.
5. Off the heat, toss in 60 g Parmigiano-Reggiano, finely grated and splashes of pasta water until glossy. Add the rest of the lemon juice to taste.
6. Fold in the chicken and its juices with 10 g parsley, chopped and the rest of the pepper, and serve.

Notes
The starch in the pasta water and the Parmigiano make the sauce creamy without any cream. Add the cheese off the heat so it doesn't clump. A handful of baby spinach or arugula tossed in at the end adds green.
