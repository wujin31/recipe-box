Ribeye Steak Sandwich — with Caramelized Onions, Provolone and Horseradish Mayo

A dry-brined ribeye seared hard and sliced thin, piled on toasted ciabatta with jammy onions, melted provolone, peppery arugula and a sharp horseradish mayo. Serves 2.

Ingredients
• 450 g boneless ribeye steak, about 2.5 cm thick
• 1 tsp kosher salt
• 1/2 tsp black pepper
• 1 tbsp neutral oil
• 1 tbsp butter
• 1 large yellow onion, thinly sliced
• 1 tbsp butter (for the onions)
• 1/4 tsp salt (for the onions)
• 3 tbsp mayonnaise
• 1 tbsp prepared horseradish
• 1 tsp Dijon mustard
• 2 ciabatta rolls, split
• 4 slices provolone
• 30 g arugula

Steps
1. Season 450 g boneless ribeye steak, about 2.5 cm thick all over with 1 tsp kosher salt and refrigerate uncovered at least 45 minutes (or up to overnight).
2. Cook 1 large yellow onion, thinly sliced with 1 tbsp butter (for the onions) and 1/4 tsp salt (for the onions) in a skillet over medium-low for 25 minutes, stirring now and then, until deep golden and jammy. Set aside.
3. Mix 3 tbsp mayonnaise, 1 tbsp prepared horseradish and 1 tsp Dijon mustard.
4. Pat the steak dry. Heat 1 tbsp neutral oil in a cast-iron pan over high until smoking. Sear the steak 4 minutes per side, flipping every 1 minute for an even crust, until 52°C inside for medium-rare. Add 1 tbsp butter for the last 1 minute and baste. Season with 1/2 tsp black pepper and rest 10 minutes.
5. Toast the cut sides of 2 ciabatta rolls, split in the steak pan for 1 minute, until golden.
6. Slice the steak thinly against the grain. Pile it on the bottom halves with the onions and 4 slices provolone, and broil 1 minute to melt the cheese.
7. Spread the tops with horseradish mayo, add 30 g arugula, close and cut in half.

Notes
Slicing thin against the grain is what makes a steak sandwich bite cleanly instead of pulling out in one piece. Drizzle the resting juices over the meat before closing the sandwich.
