Carnitas Michoacanas — Lard-Simmered Pork

Quiroga-style carnitas: pork shoulder confited in lard with orange, garlic and bay until tender, then browned in the same fat with a splash of Mexican Coke and milk until crisp-edged and golden. Serves 8.

Ingredients
• 2 kg boneless pork shoulder, cut into 6 cm chunks
• 1 tbsp salt
• 1 kg lard
• 1 orange, halved
• 1 white onion, halved
• 6 garlic cloves, smashed
• 3 bay leaves
• 1 tsp Mexican oregano
• 120 ml Mexican Coca-Cola
• 120 ml whole milk
• 24 corn tortillas, warmed
• 1 white onion, finely chopped (to serve)
• 1 bunch cilantro, chopped
• 250 ml salsa verde
• 4 limes, cut into wedges

Steps
1. Toss 2 kg boneless pork shoulder, cut into 6 cm chunks with 1 tbsp salt and leave 30 minutes while you prepare everything else.
2. Melt 1 kg lard in a large heavy Dutch oven over medium. Add the pork; it should be mostly submerged. Squeeze in the juice of 1 orange, halved and drop in the rinds, with 1 white onion, halved, 6 garlic cloves, smashed, 3 bay leaves and 1 tsp Mexican oregano.
3. Cook at a gentle bubble, uncovered, for 2 hours 30 minutes, stirring now and then, until the pork is tender enough to break with a spoon. Adjust the heat to keep small, lazy bubbles.
4. Pour in 120 ml Mexican Coca-Cola and 120 ml whole milk slowly and carefully (it will sputter), and cook 20 minutes, until the liquid has evaporated and the pork begins to brown.
5. Raise the heat to medium-high and fry 10 minutes, turning, until the outside is deep golden and crisp at the edges.
6. Lift out the pork with a slotted spoon, drain, and chop or pull into pieces, mixing crisp and tender bits. Serve with 24 corn tortillas, warmed, 1 white onion, finely chopped (to serve), 1 bunch cilantro, chopped, 250 ml salsa verde and 4 limes, cut into wedges.

Notes
The milk and Coke are Michoacán touches: their sugars brown the pork and give the signature color. Strain and refrigerate the lard afterward; it's seasoned for refried beans or the next batch.

In Michoacán, carnitas are a mix of cuts: add ribs, skin (cueritos) or belly for variety. Without enough lard, use Diana Kennedy's method: just cover the pork with water and salt, simmer uncovered until the water evaporates (about 1 hr 30 min), then fry it in its own rendered fat for 20 minutes.
