Shakshuka — Eggs Poached in Spiced Tomato and Pepper Sauce

The North African dish adopted across Israel and the Middle East: onion, red peppers and garlic stewed with cumin, paprika and harissa into a thick tomato sauce, with eggs nestled in to poach. Serves 3.

Ingredients
• 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
• 1 onion, thinly sliced
• 2 red bell peppers, thinly sliced
• 3/4 tsp salt
• 4 garlic cloves, sliced
• 1 tsp ground cumin
• 2 tsp sweet paprika
• 1 tbsp harissa (or 1/2 tsp cayenne)
• 1 tbsp tomato paste
• 800 g canned whole peeled tomatoes, crushed by hand
• 1/2 tsp sugar
• 6 eggs
• 60 g feta, crumbled (optional)
• 10 g cilantro or parsley, chopped
• 1 loaf crusty bread or pita, to serve

Steps
1. Heat 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil in a 28 cm skillet over medium. Cook 1 onion, thinly sliced, 2 red bell peppers, thinly sliced and 3/4 tsp salt for 12 minutes, stirring, until very soft and starting to brown.
2. Add 4 garlic cloves, sliced, 1 tsp ground cumin, 2 tsp sweet paprika, 1 tbsp harissa (or 1/2 tsp cayenne) and 1 tbsp tomato paste and stir 1 minute, until fragrant and brick red.
3. Add 800 g canned whole peeled tomatoes, crushed by hand and 1/2 tsp sugar and simmer 15 minutes, stirring now and then, until thick enough that a spoon leaves a trail. Taste and adjust salt and heat.
4. Make 6 wells in the sauce and crack one of 6 eggs into each. Cover and cook on medium-low for 7 minutes, until the whites are set but the yolks still runny.
5. Scatter with 60 g feta, crumbled (optional) and 10 g cilantro or parsley, chopped and serve straight from the pan with 1 loaf crusty bread or pita, to serve.

Notes
Cook the sauce down properly; watery sauce makes watery eggs. Spoon a little sauce over the edges of the whites to help them set before the yolks overcook. Tunisian versions often add preserved lemon or merguez sausage; Israeli cafés serve it with tahini.
