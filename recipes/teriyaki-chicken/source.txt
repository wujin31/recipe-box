照り焼きチキン (Teriyaki Chikin) · Chicken Teriyaki with Rice

Real teriyaki has no bottled sauce: crisp-skinned chicken thigh glazed in the pan with equal parts soy, sake and mirin plus a little sugar, reduced until lacquered. Salmon variation in the notes. Serves 2.

Ingredients
• 2 boneless skin-on chicken thighs (about 500 g)
• 1/4 tsp salt
• 1 tsp neutral oil
• 2 tbsp soy sauce
• 2 tbsp sake
• 2 tbsp mirin
• 1 tbsp sugar
• 450 g hot cooked short-grain rice
• 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds
• 1 scallion, thinly sliced

Steps
1. Pat 2 boneless skin-on chicken thighs (about 500 g) dry, prick the skin all over with a fork, and season with 1/4 tsp salt. Mix 2 tbsp soy sauce, 2 tbsp sake, 2 tbsp mirin and 1 tbsp sugar.
2. Heat 1 tsp neutral oil in a skillet over medium and lay the chicken in skin side down. Press with a spatula and cook 7 minutes, until the skin is deep golden and crisp.
3. Flip and cook 3 minutes. Wipe out the rendered fat with paper towels.
4. Pour in the sauce and let it bubble for 2 minutes, spooning it over the chicken and turning it, until it reduces to a thick, shiny glaze.
5. Slice and serve over 450 g hot cooked short-grain rice, spooning the glaze over, with 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds and 1 scallion, thinly sliced.

Notes
Teriyaki means "shine-grilled": the glaze comes from reducing the sauce on the meat, not from cornstarch. Wiping out the fat lets the sauce reduce cleanly instead of frying.

Salmon: use 2 fillets (about 300 g), salt them 10 minutes, pat dry and dust lightly with flour. Fry skin side down 3 minutes and the other side 2 minutes, then glaze the same way for 1 minute. Serve with blanched broccoli or a cucumber sunomono.
