Soy-Glazed Salmon Bowl

Pan-seared salmon lacquered in a soy, honey and ginger glaze, over rice with quick-pickled cucumber, avocado and scallions. 25 minutes. Serves 2.

Ingredients
• 2 skin-on salmon fillets (about 300 g)
• 1/4 tsp salt
• 1 tsp neutral oil
• 2 tbsp soy sauce
• 1 tbsp honey
• 1 tbsp mirin
• 1 tsp grated ginger
• 1 garlic clove, grated
• 1 Persian cucumber, thinly sliced
• 1 tbsp rice vinegar
• 1/2 tsp sugar
• 1/4 tsp salt (for the cucumber)
• 450 g hot cooked short-grain rice
• 1 avocado, sliced
• 2 scallions, thinly sliced
• 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds

Steps
1. Toss 1 Persian cucumber, thinly sliced with 1 tbsp rice vinegar, 1/2 tsp sugar and 1/4 tsp salt (for the cucumber) and set aside for 10 minutes.
2. Mix 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp honey, 1 tbsp mirin, 1 tsp grated ginger and 1 garlic clove, grated.
3. Pat 2 skin-on salmon fillets (about 300 g) dry and season with 1/4 tsp salt. Heat 1 tsp neutral oil in a nonstick skillet over medium-high and cook the salmon skin side down for 5 minutes, until the skin is crisp. Flip and cook 1 minute.
4. Lower the heat to medium, pour in the glaze and cook 1 minute, spooning it over the salmon, until thick and shiny.
5. Divide 450 g hot cooked short-grain rice between bowls and top with the salmon, the drained cucumber, 1 avocado, sliced, 2 scallions, thinly sliced and 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds. Drizzle with the pan glaze.

Notes
Take the salmon off when the center is still slightly translucent (about 50°C); it carries over to medium as it sits. Kimchi, edamame or a fried egg are easy extras.
