Coconut-Miso Salmon Curry

A brothy, gentle curry: onion, ginger and garlic softened with white miso until it caramelizes, simmered with coconut milk, then salmon poached in it and finished with spinach, lime and lots of herbs. Serves 4.

Ingredients
• 2 tbsp neutral oil
• 1 red onion, halved and thickly sliced
• 30 g ginger, minced
• 3 garlic cloves, minced
• 1/2 tsp salt
• 1/4 tsp black pepper
• 60 g white miso (1/4 cup)
• 120 ml full-fat coconut milk
• 720 ml water
• 600 g skinless salmon fillet, cut into 5 cm pieces
• 140 g baby spinach
• 1 tbsp lime juice, plus wedges to serve
• 15 g basil leaves, torn
• 15 g cilantro, chopped
• 900 g hot cooked rice

Steps
1. Heat 2 tbsp neutral oil in a large pot over medium. Add 1 red onion, halved and thickly sliced, 30 g ginger, minced, 3 garlic cloves, minced, 1/2 tsp salt and 1/4 tsp black pepper and cook 3 minutes, until softened.
2. Add 60 g white miso (1/4 cup) and cook 2 minutes, stirring, until it starts to caramelize on the bottom of the pot.
3. Add 120 ml full-fat coconut milk and 720 ml water, scraping up the miso, bring to a boil and simmer 5 minutes, until slightly reduced.
4. Add 600 g skinless salmon fillet, cut into 5 cm pieces, lower the heat to medium-low and simmer gently for 5 minutes, until just cooked through.
5. Turn off the heat and stir in 140 g baby spinach and 1 tbsp lime juice, plus wedges to serve. Season with salt.
6. Spoon 900 g hot cooked rice into bowls, ladle over the curry, and top with 15 g basil leaves, torn and 15 g cilantro, chopped. Serve with lime wedges.

Notes
Adapted from Kay Chun's NYT Cooking recipe. Letting the miso brown before adding liquid is what gives the broth its depth. Don't boil once the salmon is in; a gentle simmer keeps it silky. Cod, halibut or tofu work the same way.
