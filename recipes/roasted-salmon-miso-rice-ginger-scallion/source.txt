Roasted Salmon with Miso Rice and Ginger-Scallion Vinaigrette

Rice cooked with white miso whisked into its water for a savory, buttery depth, topped with simply roasted salmon and a bright, sharp vinaigrette of scallion, ginger and rice vinegar. Serves 4.

Ingredients
For the miso rice:
• 300 g short-grain rice, rinsed
• 2 tbsp white miso
• 400 ml water
• 1 tbsp butter
For the salmon:
• 4 skin-on salmon fillets (about 170 g each)
• 1 tbsp neutral oil
• 3/4 tsp salt
• 1/4 tsp black pepper
For the vinaigrette:
• 4 scallions, finely chopped
• 2 tbsp finely grated ginger
• 3 tbsp rice vinegar
• 1 tbsp soy sauce
• 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
• 3 tbsp neutral oil (for the vinaigrette)
• 1 tsp sugar
To serve:
• 1 English cucumber, sliced

Steps
1. Whisk 2 tbsp white miso into 400 ml water until dissolved. Combine with 300 g short-grain rice, rinsed and 1 tbsp butter in a rice cooker or heavy pot. Cook on the white rice setting (about 45 minutes), or bring to a boil, cover, and cook on low for 15 minutes and rest 10 minutes off the heat.
2. Heat the oven to 220°C. Rub 4 skin-on salmon fillets (about 170 g each) with 1 tbsp neutral oil and season with 3/4 tsp salt and 1/4 tsp black pepper. Roast skin side down on a lined sheet pan for 10 minutes, until just opaque in the center.
3. Stir together 4 scallions, finely chopped, 2 tbsp finely grated ginger, 3 tbsp rice vinegar, 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tsp toasted sesame oil, 3 tbsp neutral oil (for the vinaigrette) and 1 tsp sugar.
4. Fluff the rice and divide among plates. Top with the salmon and spoon over the vinaigrette. Serve with 1 English cucumber, sliced.

Notes
Adapted from Kay Chun's NYT Cooking recipe. Miso can scorch at the bottom of a pot, so stir it fully into the water before cooking. The vinaigrette keeps a few days and is great on grilled steak, chicken or tofu.
