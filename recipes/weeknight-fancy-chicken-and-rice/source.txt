Weeknight Fancy Chicken and Rice — One-Pan Saffron Chicken with Herbs

Crisp-skinned chicken thighs baked on top of saffron and lemon rice that cooks in the chicken drippings, finished with a pile of fresh herbs, toasted almonds and garlicky yogurt. One pan. Serves 4.

Ingredients
• 8 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (about 1.4 kg)
• 2 tsp kosher salt
• 1/2 tsp black pepper
• 1 tsp ground cumin
• 1 tbsp olive oil
• 1 onion, finely chopped
• 4 garlic cloves, minced
• 300 g basmati rice, rinsed
• 1 large pinch saffron, crumbled into 2 tbsp hot water
• 600 ml chicken stock
• 1 lemon, zested, then cut into wedges
• 30 g mixed herbs (parsley, dill, cilantro, mint), chopped
• 40 g sliced almonds, toasted
• 200 g plain Greek yogurt
• 1 small garlic clove, grated (for the yogurt)

Steps
1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Pat 8 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (about 1.4 kg) dry and season with 2 tsp kosher salt, 1/2 tsp black pepper and 1 tsp ground cumin.
2. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large ovenproof skillet or braiser over medium-high. Cook the thighs skin side down for 8 minutes, until the skin is deep golden and crisp. Transfer to a plate skin side up. Pour off all but 2 tbsp fat.
3. Lower the heat to medium. Cook 1 onion, finely chopped for 5 minutes, until soft. Add 4 garlic cloves, minced and 300 g basmati rice, rinsed and stir 2 minutes, until the grains are coated and translucent at the edges.
4. Add 600 ml chicken stock, 1 large pinch saffron, crumbled into 2 tbsp hot water with its water and the zest of 1 lemon, zested, then cut into wedges. Bring to a simmer and season with a pinch of salt.
5. Nestle the chicken on top, skin side up and above the liquid. Bake uncovered for 35 minutes, until the rice is tender, the liquid absorbed and the chicken 80°C at the bone.
6. Rest 10 minutes. Stir 1 small garlic clove, grated (for the yogurt) and a pinch of salt into 200 g plain Greek yogurt. Scatter 30 g mixed herbs (parsley, dill, cilantro, mint), chopped and 40 g sliced almonds, toasted over the pan and serve with the yogurt and lemon wedges.

Notes
Keep the chicken skin above the liquid so it stays crisp in the oven. The crust that forms on the bottom of the pan is a bonus, like a tahdig. Golden raisins or barberries stirred into the rice are a nice Persian-leaning touch.
