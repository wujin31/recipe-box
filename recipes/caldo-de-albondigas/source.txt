Caldo de Albóndigas — Mexican Meatball Soup with Mint

Soft beef and pork meatballs studded with rice and hierbabuena, simmered in a light tomato broth with carrot, potato and zucchini. Serves 6.

Ingredients
For the meatballs:
• 450 g ground beef
• 225 g ground pork
• 60 g long-grain white rice, rinsed
• 1 egg
• 1/4 white onion, minced
• 1 garlic clove, minced
• 2 tbsp fresh mint (hierbabuena), chopped
• 1/2 tsp ground cumin
• 1 tsp salt (for the meatballs)
• 1/4 tsp black pepper
For the broth:
• 3 Roma tomatoes
• 1/4 white onion
• 2 garlic cloves
• 1 tbsp neutral oil
• 2 L chicken stock
• 2 sprigs fresh mint
• 2 carrots, sliced
• 1 large potato, cubed
• 2 Mexican squash or zucchini, cubed
• 1 tsp salt, to taste
To serve:
• 2 limes, cut into wedges
• 12 corn tortillas, warmed

Steps
1. Mix 450 g ground beef, 225 g ground pork, 60 g long-grain white rice, rinsed, 1 egg, 1/4 white onion, minced, 1 garlic clove, minced, 2 tbsp fresh mint (hierbabuena), chopped, 1/2 tsp ground cumin, 1 tsp salt (for the meatballs) and 1/4 tsp black pepper with your hands until well combined. Roll into 4 cm balls (about 24).
2. Blend 3 Roma tomatoes, 1/4 white onion and 2 garlic cloves with 120 ml water until smooth.
3. Heat 1 tbsp neutral oil in a large pot over medium and fry the tomato purée for 5 minutes, until it darkens slightly. Add 2 L chicken stock and 2 sprigs fresh mint and bring to a boil.
4. Lower in the meatballs one at a time and simmer gently, uncovered, for 10 minutes without stirring so they set.
5. Add 2 carrots, sliced and 1 large potato, cubed and simmer 15 minutes. Add 2 Mexican squash or zucchini, cubed and simmer 10 minutes more, until the vegetables are tender and the rice in the meatballs is cooked. Season with 1 tsp salt, to taste.
6. Serve in deep bowls with 2 limes, cut into wedges and 12 corn tortillas, warmed.

Notes
Raw rice cooks inside the meatballs as they simmer, keeping them light. The mint (hierbabuena, spearmint) is the signature; don't skip it. A chipotle in adobo blended with the tomatoes is a common smoky variation.
