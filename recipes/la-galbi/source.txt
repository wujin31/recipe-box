갈비 (Galbi) · LA-Style Korean BBQ Short Ribs

Thin cross-cut (flanken) beef short ribs marinated overnight in soy, Asian pear, onion and garlic, then grilled hot and fast until caramelized at the edges. Serves 4.

Ingredients
• 1.4 kg flanken-cut beef short ribs (LA galbi), 1 cm thick
• 80 ml soy sauce
• 80 ml rice wine or water
• 60 ml honey or 70 g brown sugar
• 1 tsp ground black pepper
• 1 Korean (Asian) pear, peeled and chopped
• 1 onion, chopped
• 8 garlic cloves
• 1 tsp minced ginger
• 2 tbsp toasted sesame oil
• 2 scallions, chopped
• 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
• 1 head red-leaf lettuce, leaves separated (to serve)
• 900 g hot cooked short-grain rice

Steps
1. Soak 1.4 kg flanken-cut beef short ribs (LA galbi), 1 cm thick in cold water for 30 minutes to draw out blood and bone fragments, then rinse each piece and drain well.
2. Blend 1 Korean (Asian) pear, peeled and chopped, 1 onion, chopped, 8 garlic cloves and 1 tsp minced ginger until smooth and creamy. Stir in 80 ml soy sauce, 80 ml rice wine or water, 60 ml honey or 70 g brown sugar, 1 tsp ground black pepper and 2 tbsp toasted sesame oil.
3. Layer the ribs in a container, pouring marinade over each layer. Cover and refrigerate at least 8 hours or overnight (up to 24 hours).
4. Heat a grill or a cast-iron pan to high. Shake off excess marinade and cook the ribs 3 minutes per side, until charred at the edges and just cooked through. Work in batches; don't crowd the pan.
5. Cut between the bones into pieces with kitchen scissors, sprinkle with 2 scallions, chopped and 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds, and serve with 1 head red-leaf lettuce, leaves separated (to serve) for wrapping and 900 g hot cooked short-grain rice.

Notes
The pear's enzyme tenderizes the meat; don't go past 24 hours or the texture turns mushy. Kiwi works the same way but much faster: use 1/2 kiwi in place of the pear and marinate only 2 to 4 hours.

The sugar burns quickly; if the outside chars before the inside cooks, lower the heat a notch. Serve with ssamjang, kimchi and banchan.
