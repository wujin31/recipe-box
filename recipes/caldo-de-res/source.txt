Caldo de Res — Mexican Beef and Vegetable Soup

Bone-in beef shank simmered into a clear, beefy broth, then loaded with corn on the cob, carrots, potatoes, chayote, zucchini and cabbage. Served with lime, onion, cilantro and rice. Serves 6.

Ingredients
For the broth:
• 1.5 kg bone-in beef shank (chambarete), cut into thick slices
• 4 L water
• 1/2 white onion
• 1 head garlic, halved
• 2 bay leaves
• 1 tsp black peppercorns
• 1 tbsp salt
For the vegetables:
• 2 Roma tomatoes
• 3 ears corn, cut into 5 cm pieces
• 3 carrots, cut into chunks
• 3 potatoes, quartered
• 2 chayotes, cut into wedges
• 2 Mexican squash or zucchini, cut into chunks
• 1/4 green cabbage, cut into wedges
• 4 sprigs cilantro
To serve:
• 1/2 white onion, finely chopped
• 1/2 bunch cilantro, chopped
• 2 serrano chiles, chopped
• 3 limes, cut into wedges
• 600 g Mexican red rice or white rice
• 12 corn tortillas, warmed

Steps
1. Put 1.5 kg bone-in beef shank (chambarete), cut into thick slices, 4 L water, 1/2 white onion, 1 head garlic, halved, 2 bay leaves, 1 tsp black peppercorns and 1 tbsp salt in a large pot. Bring to a boil, skimming the foam for the first 10 minutes, then simmer gently, partly covered, for 2 hours, until the meat is tender.
2. Blend 2 Roma tomatoes with a ladle of broth and stir it into the pot.
3. Add 3 ears corn, cut into 5 cm pieces, 3 carrots, cut into chunks and 3 potatoes, quartered and simmer 15 minutes.
4. Add 2 chayotes, cut into wedges and simmer 10 minutes, then add 2 Mexican squash or zucchini, cut into chunks, 1/4 green cabbage, cut into wedges and 4 sprigs cilantro and simmer 8 minutes more, until everything is tender. Taste for salt.
5. Serve a piece of meat and some of every vegetable in each bowl with plenty of broth. Pass 1/2 white onion, finely chopped, 1/2 bunch cilantro, chopped, 2 serrano chiles, chopped, 3 limes, cut into wedges, 600 g Mexican red rice or white rice and 12 corn tortillas, warmed at the table.

Notes
A gentle simmer and skimming keep the broth clear. The marrow in the shank bones is a prize: spread it on a tortilla with salt. Many families spoon rice straight into the bowl. It's even better the next day.
