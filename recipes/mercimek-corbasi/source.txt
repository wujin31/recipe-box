Mercimek Çorbası — Turkish Red Lentil Soup

Turkey's everyday soup: red lentils simmered with onion, carrot and potato into a smooth, velvety purée, finished with lemon and a sizzle of butter bloomed with dried mint and pul biber. Serves 6.

Ingredients
For the soup:
• 2 tbsp olive oil
• 1 onion, chopped
• 1 carrot, chopped
• 1 small potato, peeled and chopped
• 2 garlic cloves
• 1 tbsp tomato paste or Turkish pepper paste (biber salçası)
• 1 tsp ground cumin
• 250 g red lentils, rinsed
• 1.5 L chicken or vegetable stock (or water)
• 1 tsp salt, to taste
• 1/2 tsp black pepper
For the finish:
• 3 tbsp butter
• 1 tsp dried mint
• 1 tsp pul biber (Aleppo pepper) or sweet paprika
• 2 lemons, cut into wedges

Steps
1. Heat 2 tbsp olive oil in a large pot over medium. Cook 1 onion, chopped, 1 carrot, chopped and 1 small potato, peeled and chopped for 6 minutes, until the onion is soft. Add 2 garlic cloves, 1 tbsp tomato paste or Turkish pepper paste (biber salçası) and 1 tsp ground cumin and stir 1 minute.
2. Add 250 g red lentils, rinsed and 1.5 L chicken or vegetable stock (or water), bring to a boil and skim. Simmer partly covered for 25 minutes, until the lentils have collapsed and the vegetables are soft.
3. Blend until completely smooth. Season with 1 tsp salt, to taste and 1/2 tsp black pepper, adding water if it's thicker than heavy cream.
4. Melt 3 tbsp butter in a small pan until foaming, about 1 minute. Off the heat, stir in 1 tsp dried mint and 1 tsp pul biber (Aleppo pepper) or sweet paprika.
5. Ladle the soup into bowls, drizzle with the red butter, and serve with 2 lemons, cut into wedges to squeeze in.

Notes
The lemon is not a garnish: a good squeeze is how it's eaten in Turkey. A tablespoon of rice or bulgur cooked with the lentils is a common addition for body. Serve with crusty bread.
