Lasagne alla Bolognese — Classic Lasagna with Ragù and Béchamel

The lasagna of Bologna, as codified by the Accademia Italiana della Cucina: thin egg pasta layered with slow-cooked ragù bolognese, béchamel and Parmigiano-Reggiano. No ricotta, no mozzarella. Serves 8.

Ingredients
For the ragù:
• 150 g pancetta, finely chopped
• 2 tbsp olive oil
• 1 onion, finely chopped
• 1 carrot, finely chopped
• 1 celery stalk, finely chopped
• 500 g coarsely ground beef chuck
• 250 g ground pork
• 180 ml dry white wine
• 400 g tomato passata
• 1 tbsp tomato paste
• 500 ml beef or chicken stock
• 180 ml whole milk
• 1 1/2 tsp salt
• 1/2 tsp black pepper
For the béchamel:
• 80 g butter
• 80 g all-purpose flour
• 1 L whole milk, warmed
• 1/4 tsp grated nutmeg
• 1 tsp salt (for the béchamel)
To assemble:
• 500 g fresh egg lasagna sheets (or spinach sheets for lasagne verdi)
• 150 g Parmigiano-Reggiano, grated
• 2 tbsp butter, in small pieces

Steps
1. Cook 150 g pancetta, finely chopped with 2 tbsp olive oil in a heavy pot over medium for 5 minutes, until the fat renders. Add 1 onion, finely chopped, 1 carrot, finely chopped and 1 celery stalk, finely chopped and cook 10 minutes, until soft and golden.
2. Raise the heat, add 500 g coarsely ground beef chuck and 250 g ground pork and cook 10 minutes, breaking it up, until the liquid has evaporated and the meat browns.
3. Add 180 ml dry white wine and simmer 5 minutes, until evaporated. Stir in 400 g tomato passata, 1 tbsp tomato paste and half of 500 ml beef or chicken stock.
4. Simmer uncovered on the lowest heat for 2 hours 30 minutes, adding stock as it thickens. Stir in 180 ml whole milk for the last 30 minutes. Season with 1 1/2 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp black pepper. It should be thick and rich, not soupy.
5. Make the béchamel: melt 80 g butter over medium, whisk in 80 g all-purpose flour and cook 2 minutes. Whisk in 1 L whole milk, warmed gradually and simmer 8 minutes, whisking, until it coats a spoon. Season with 1/4 tsp grated nutmeg and 1 tsp salt (for the béchamel).
6. Heat the oven to 180°C. If the sheets of 500 g fresh egg lasagna sheets (or spinach sheets for lasagne verdi) are fresh, blanch them 30 seconds in boiling salted water and lay them on towels.
7. In a 23 × 33 cm dish, spread a thin layer of béchamel, then layer pasta, ragù, béchamel and a handful of 150 g Parmigiano-Reggiano, grated. Repeat for 5 or 6 layers, finishing with béchamel and Parmigiano. Dot with 2 tbsp butter, in small pieces.
8. Bake 40 minutes, until bubbling and golden-brown on top. Rest 20 minutes before cutting.

Notes
In Bologna the layers are thin and many: a light hand with each sauce makes a lasagna that holds its shape. The milk in the ragù softens the acidity and tenderizes the meat.

Dried lasagna sheets work: use no-boil sheets or boil regular ones 2 minutes less than the package says. The whole dish can be assembled a day ahead and baked from the fridge (add 15 minutes). The ragù alone, with tagliatelle, is the real tagliatelle alla bolognese.
