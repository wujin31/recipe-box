Sticky Miso Salmon Bowl

Salmon tossed in a sweet-salty marinade of white miso, honey, ginger and citrus, broiled until glossy and charred at the edges, over rice with cucumber, avocado and pickled ginger. 30 minutes. Serves 4.

Ingredients
• 3 tbsp white miso
• 2 tbsp honey
• 1 tbsp neutral oil
• 1 tbsp grated ginger
• 1 grapefruit or orange, zested and juiced (about 60 ml juice)
• 700 g skinless salmon fillet, cut into 4 cm pieces
• 1/4 tsp salt
• 900 g hot cooked short-grain rice
• 2 Persian cucumbers, sliced
• 1 avocado, sliced
• 2 tbsp pickled sushi ginger
• 2 scallions, thinly sliced
• 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds

Steps
1. Set a rack 12 cm from the broiler and heat the broiler to high.
2. Whisk 3 tbsp white miso, 2 tbsp honey, 1 tbsp neutral oil, 1 tbsp grated ginger and 1 grapefruit or orange, zested and juiced (about 60 ml juice) in a large bowl. Season 700 g skinless salmon fillet, cut into 4 cm pieces with 1/4 tsp salt, add it to the bowl and toss to coat. Marinate 15 minutes.
3. Spread the salmon on a foil-lined sheet pan and spoon over any marinade left in the bowl.
4. Broil 6 minutes, until glossy and charred in spots and just cooked through.
5. Divide 900 g hot cooked short-grain rice among bowls and top with the salmon, 2 Persian cucumbers, sliced, 1 avocado, sliced, 2 tbsp pickled sushi ginger, 2 scallions, thinly sliced and 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds. Spoon the pan juices over the rice.

Notes
Inspired by the NYT Cooking recipe. Watch the broiler closely; the honey and miso go from caramelized to burnt fast. Small pieces get more of the sticky, charred surface than whole fillets.
