肉末燒豆腐 (Ròumò Shāo Dòufu) · Soy-Braised Tofu with Ground Pork

Everyday Taiwanese home cooking: firm tofu pan-fried until golden, then simmered with ground pork in a soy and oyster sauce braise until it drinks up the sauce. Serves 3.

Ingredients
• 450 g firm tofu, cut into 2 cm thick squares
• 2 tbsp neutral oil
• 150 g ground pork
• 3 garlic cloves, minced
• 10 g ginger, minced
• 3 scallions, whites and greens sliced separately
• 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine
• 2 tbsp soy sauce
• 1 tbsp oyster sauce
• 1/2 tsp dark soy sauce
• 1 tsp sugar
• 1/4 tsp ground white pepper
• 180 ml water or chicken stock
• 1 tsp cornstarch
• 1 tbsp water (for the slurry)
• 1/2 tsp sesame oil

Steps
1. Pat 450 g firm tofu, cut into 2 cm thick squares very dry with paper towels.
2. Heat 2 tbsp neutral oil in a nonstick or well-seasoned pan over medium-high. Fry the tofu 3 minutes per side, until golden, then set aside.
3. Add 150 g ground pork to the same pan and break it up. Cook 3 minutes, until browned. Add 3 garlic cloves, minced, 10 g ginger, minced and the scallion whites from 3 scallions, whites and greens sliced separately and stir 30 seconds.
4. Pour in 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine, then add 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp oyster sauce, 1/2 tsp dark soy sauce, 1 tsp sugar, 1/4 tsp ground white pepper and 180 ml water or chicken stock. Return the tofu, bring to a boil and simmer over medium-low for 8 minutes, turning the tofu once, until the sauce has reduced by half.
5. Stir 1 tsp cornstarch into 1 tbsp water (for the slurry), pour it in and simmer 30 seconds until glossy. Drizzle with 1/2 tsp sesame oil, scatter the scallion greens and serve with rice.

Notes
Frying the tofu first gives it a skin that holds together and soaks up sauce like a sponge. A few sliced rehydrated shiitake (add with the pork) or a spoon of doubanjiang (with the aromatics, for 家常豆腐 style) are common additions.
