Huli Huli Chicken — Hawaiian Grilled Chicken with Pineapple-Shoyu Glaze

The roadside fundraiser classic: chicken marinated in shoyu, pineapple juice, brown sugar, ketchup and ginger, grilled and turned ("huli" means turn) while basted with the reduced glaze. Serves 6.

Ingredients
• 1.6 kg bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs and drumsticks
• 240 ml pineapple juice
• 120 ml shoyu (soy sauce)
• 110 g brown sugar
• 80 ml ketchup
• 2 tbsp rice vinegar
• 2 tbsp grated ginger
• 4 garlic cloves, grated
• 1 tsp sriracha (optional)
• 1 tbsp neutral oil (for the grill)
• 3 scallions, sliced

Steps
1. Whisk 240 ml pineapple juice, 120 ml shoyu (soy sauce), 110 g brown sugar, 80 ml ketchup, 2 tbsp rice vinegar, 2 tbsp grated ginger, 4 garlic cloves, grated and 1 tsp sriracha (optional). Set aside 250 ml for the glaze.
2. Put 1.6 kg bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs and drumsticks in a zip bag with the rest of the marinade and refrigerate at least 4 hours or overnight.
3. Simmer the reserved sauce in a small pan for 10 minutes, until reduced by about half and syrupy.
4. Set up a grill for two-zone cooking (one side hot, one side off) and oil the grates with 1 tbsp neutral oil (for the grill). Grill the chicken skin side up over indirect heat, lid closed, for 30 minutes, turning once.
5. Move the chicken over direct heat and grill 8 minutes, turning every 2 minutes and brushing with the glaze each time, until lacquered, charred in spots and 80°C at the bone.
6. Rest 5 minutes, brush with the last of the glaze and scatter with 3 scallions, sliced. Serve with rice and mac salad.

Notes
The sugar in the marinade burns over direct heat, which is why the chicken cooks through on the cool side first and only gets glazed at the end. Oven method: bake at 200°C for 35 minutes, then broil 5 minutes, brushing with glaze every 2 minutes.

Boneless thighs work too: grill 5 minutes per side over medium-direct heat, then glaze. Mesquite or kiawe wood chips get closer to the roadside flavor.
