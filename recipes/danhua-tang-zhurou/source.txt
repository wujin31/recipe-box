豬肉蛋花湯 (Zhūròu Dànhuā Tāng) · Egg Drop Soup with Ground Pork

A light, silky Chinese home-style soup: seasoned ground pork poached in ginger-scented chicken stock, lightly thickened, with ribbons of egg drizzled in at the end. 15 minutes. Serves 4.

Ingredients
• 150 g ground pork
• 1 tsp soy sauce (for the pork)
• 1/2 tsp cornstarch (for the pork)
• 1 tsp Shaoxing wine
• 1.2 L chicken stock
• 3 slices ginger
• 2 tbsp cornstarch mixed with 3 tbsp water
• 3 eggs, beaten
• 1/2 tsp salt, to taste
• 1/4 tsp ground white pepper
• 1 tsp sesame oil
• 2 scallions, thinly sliced

Steps
1. Mix 150 g ground pork with 1 tsp soy sauce (for the pork), 1/2 tsp cornstarch (for the pork) and 1 tsp Shaoxing wine.
2. Bring 1.2 L chicken stock and 3 slices ginger to a boil. Drop in the pork in small pinches and simmer 3 minutes, skimming any foam, until cooked through.
3. Stir 2 tbsp cornstarch mixed with 3 tbsp water and drizzle it in while stirring. Simmer 30 seconds, until slightly thickened. Season with 1/2 tsp salt, to taste and 1/4 tsp ground white pepper.
4. Turn the heat to low. Stir the soup slowly in one direction and pour 3 eggs, beaten in a thin stream from high up, so they set into ribbons. Leave 10 seconds before stirring again.
5. Turn off the heat, add 1 tsp sesame oil and 2 scallions, thinly sliced, and serve.

Notes
Thickening the soup before adding the egg is what suspends the ribbons rather than letting them sink into clumps. For 番茄蛋花湯 (tomato egg drop), add 2 diced tomatoes with the pork; for a silken tofu version, add 200 g cubed tofu after the pork.
