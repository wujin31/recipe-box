Miso Butter Pasta

Japanese wafu-style pasta: spaghetti glossed with white miso and butter emulsified in pasta water, with sautéed garlic, lots of black pepper, Parmesan and scallions. 20 minutes. Serves 2.

Ingredients
• 200 g spaghetti
• 1 tsp salt (for the pasta water)
• 3 tbsp unsalted butter
• 2 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
• 2 tbsp white miso
• 1 tsp soy sauce
• 1/2 tsp black pepper, coarsely ground
• 20 g Parmesan, finely grated
• 2 scallions, thinly sliced
• 1/2 sheet nori, cut into thin strips (optional)

Steps
1. Cook 200 g spaghetti in boiling water with 1 tsp salt (for the pasta water) (less salt than usual; miso is salty) 1 minute less than the package says. Reserve 240 ml pasta water.
2. Meanwhile, melt 1 tbsp of 3 tbsp unsalted butter in a large skillet over medium-low and cook 2 garlic cloves, thinly sliced for 2 minutes, until pale gold.
3. Whisk 2 tbsp white miso and 1 tsp soy sauce with 120 ml pasta water until smooth and add it to the skillet.
4. Add the pasta and the remaining butter and toss over medium heat for 2 minutes, adding pasta water as needed, until the noodles are glossy and coated.
5. Off the heat, toss in 1/2 tsp black pepper, coarsely ground and 20 g Parmesan, finely grated. Serve topped with 2 scallions, thinly sliced and 1/2 sheet nori, cut into thin strips (optional).

Notes
Dissolve the miso in water before it goes in the pan so it doesn't clump. Sautéed mushrooms, bacon or a soft egg are common additions in Japanese cafés.
