SF Garlic Noodles — San Francisco-Style Vietnamese Garlic Noodles

The Vietnamese-American classic made famous at Thanh Long and Crustacean: noodles tossed in a lot of butter and garlic with oyster sauce, fish sauce, Maggi and Parmesan, glossy and savory. 20 minutes. Serves 4.

Ingredients
• 450 g fresh egg noodles or spaghetti
• 115 g unsalted butter
• 12 garlic cloves, finely minced
• 2 tbsp oyster sauce
• 1 tbsp fish sauce
• 2 tsp Maggi seasoning or soy sauce
• 1 tsp sugar
• 40 g Parmesan, finely grated
• 3 scallions, thinly sliced

Steps
1. Cook 450 g fresh egg noodles or spaghetti in boiling water until just tender (about 2 minutes for fresh egg noodles, or per the package). Reserve 120 ml cooking water and drain.
2. Melt 115 g unsalted butter in a large skillet over medium-low. Add 12 garlic cloves, finely minced and cook 2 minutes, stirring, until fragrant and just turning pale gold.
3. Stir in 2 tbsp oyster sauce, 1 tbsp fish sauce, 2 tsp Maggi seasoning or soy sauce, 1 tsp sugar and 60 ml of the cooking water.
4. Add the noodles and 40 g Parmesan, finely grated and toss over medium heat for 1 minute, until the sauce clings to the noodles rather than pooling. Add more water if it's tight.
5. Top with 3 scallions, thinly sliced and serve, traditionally with roast Dungeness crab or garlic butter shrimp.

Notes
The garlic should be soft and fragrant, not browned; browned garlic turns bitter in this much butter. Garlic chips (thinly sliced garlic fried separately in oil until golden) scattered on top are a good restaurant-style finish.
