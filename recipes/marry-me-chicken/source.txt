Marry Me Chicken

Golden seared chicken cutlets simmered in a creamy Parmesan sauce with sun-dried tomatoes, garlic, thyme and chili flakes, finished with basil. Serves 4.

Ingredients
• 2 large boneless skinless chicken breasts (about 700 g), halved horizontally into cutlets
• 1 tsp salt
• 1/2 tsp black pepper
• 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
• 2 tbsp olive oil
• 2 tbsp butter
• 4 garlic cloves, minced
• 1 tsp fresh thyme leaves (or 1/2 tsp dried oregano)
• 1/2 tsp red chili flakes
• 1/2 tsp paprika
• 180 ml chicken stock
• 240 ml heavy cream
• 60 g oil-packed sun-dried tomatoes, chopped
• 40 g Parmigiano-Reggiano, finely grated
• 10 g basil leaves, torn

Steps
1. Season 2 large boneless skinless chicken breasts (about 700 g), halved horizontally into cutlets with 1 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp black pepper and dust lightly with 2 tbsp all-purpose flour.
2. Heat 2 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Sear the chicken 4 minutes per side, until golden and nearly cooked through. Transfer to a plate.
3. Lower the heat to medium and melt 2 tbsp butter. Add 4 garlic cloves, minced, 1 tsp fresh thyme leaves (or 1/2 tsp dried oregano), 1/2 tsp red chili flakes and 1/2 tsp paprika and stir 1 minute.
4. Pour in 180 ml chicken stock, scraping up the browned bits, and simmer 2 minutes. Add 240 ml heavy cream and 60 g oil-packed sun-dried tomatoes, chopped and simmer 3 minutes, until slightly thickened. Stir in 40 g Parmigiano-Reggiano, finely grated.
5. Return the chicken and its juices and simmer 4 minutes, spooning the sauce over, until the chicken is 74°C inside.
6. Scatter with 10 g basil leaves, torn and serve with pasta, rice or crusty bread.

Notes
Slicing the breasts into thin cutlets means they cook through in the time it takes to brown, so they stay juicy. A spoonful of the sun-dried tomato oil in place of some of the olive oil deepens the flavor.
