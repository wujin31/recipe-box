Cheesy Potato Gratin — Gratin with Gruyère and Thyme

Thin slices of Yukon Gold simmered briefly in garlic- and thyme-infused cream and milk, layered with Gruyère and baked until tender, bubbling and deeply browned. Serves 8.

Ingredients
• 1.4 kg Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled and sliced 3 mm thick
• 480 ml heavy cream
• 240 ml whole milk
• 2 garlic cloves, smashed
• 4 sprigs fresh thyme
• 1 1/2 tsp salt
• 1/2 tsp black pepper
• 1/8 tsp grated nutmeg
• 1 tbsp butter (for the dish)
• 170 g Gruyère, grated
• 30 g Parmigiano-Reggiano, grated

Steps
1. Heat the oven to 190°C and rub a 23 × 33 cm baking dish with 1 tbsp butter (for the dish).
2. Put 480 ml heavy cream, 240 ml whole milk, 2 garlic cloves, smashed, 4 sprigs fresh thyme, 1 1/2 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp black pepper and 1/8 tsp grated nutmeg in a large pot and add 1.4 kg Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled and sliced 3 mm thick. Bring to a simmer over medium, stirring gently, and cook 8 minutes, until the potatoes start to soften and the liquid thickens from their starch. Remove the garlic and thyme stems.
3. Spoon a third of the potatoes and cream into the dish, scatter with a third of 170 g Gruyère, grated, and repeat twice. Top with 30 g Parmigiano-Reggiano, grated.
4. Bake 45 minutes, until a knife slides through the potatoes easily and the top is deep golden-brown.
5. Rest 15 minutes before serving so the cream sets.

Notes
Don't rinse the sliced potatoes: their starch thickens the cream. A mandoline gives even slices that cook at the same rate. The classic gratin dauphinois has no cheese; this is closer to a gratin savoyard. It can be baked a day ahead and reheated at 180°C for 20 minutes.
