부대찌개 (Budae Jjigae) · Korean Army Base Stew

The postwar stew born from US army rations: Spam, hot dogs, kimchi, tofu and baked beans arranged in a shallow pot with a spicy gochugaru sauce, boiled at the table and finished with instant ramen and American cheese. Serves 4.

Ingredients
For the sauce:
• 2 tbsp gochugaru (Korean chili flakes)
• 1 tbsp gochujang
• 1 tbsp soy sauce
• 1 tbsp mirin
• 1 tbsp minced garlic
• 1 tsp sugar
• 1/4 tsp black pepper
For the stew:
• 1 L anchovy-kelp stock, beef stock or chicken stock
• 200 g well-fermented kimchi, chopped
• 170 g Spam (1/2 can), sliced 5 mm thick
• 2 hot dogs, sliced on the diagonal
• 200 g firm tofu, sliced 1 cm thick
• 1/2 onion, sliced
• 100 g enoki or shiitake mushrooms
• 100 g sliced rice cakes (tteok), soaked if hard
• 3 tbsp canned baked beans
• 3 scallions, cut into 5 cm lengths
• 1 pack instant ramen noodles (discard the seasoning or use half)
• 1 slice American cheese

Steps
1. Mix 2 tbsp gochugaru (Korean chili flakes), 1 tbsp gochujang, 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp mirin, 1 tbsp minced garlic, 1 tsp sugar and 1/4 tsp black pepper into a paste.
2. In a wide shallow pot, arrange 200 g well-fermented kimchi, chopped, 170 g Spam (1/2 can), sliced 5 mm thick, 2 hot dogs, sliced on the diagonal, 200 g firm tofu, sliced 1 cm thick, 1/2 onion, sliced, 100 g enoki or shiitake mushrooms and 100 g sliced rice cakes (tteok), soaked if hard in neat sections around the edge. Spoon 3 tbsp canned baked beans and the sauce into the center and lay 3 scallions, cut into 5 cm lengths on top.
3. Pour in 1 L anchovy-kelp stock, beef stock or chicken stock around the edge, bring to a boil over high heat and boil 8 minutes, gently stirring the sauce into the broth.
4. Add 1 pack instant ramen noodles (discard the seasoning or use half) and cook 3 minutes, until the noodles loosen and are just tender. Lay 1 slice American cheese on top and let it melt into the broth.
5. Serve straight from the pot at the table, ladling into bowls with rice on the side.

Notes
Anchovy-kelp stock is traditional (soak 6 dried anchovies and a 10 cm piece of kombu in 1.2 L water, boil 15 minutes, strain), but beef bone broth (sagol) is what many restaurants use. Spam and kimchi are salty, so taste before adding any salt.

A portable burner at the table is the proper way to eat it: keep it bubbling and add more stock, noodles or rice cakes as it goes.
