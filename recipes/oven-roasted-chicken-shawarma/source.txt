Oven-Roasted Chicken Shawarma

Chicken thighs marinated in lemon, garlic and a warm Levantine spice blend, roasted hot until charred at the edges, then sliced and crisped. Served in pita with garlicky tahini yogurt, pickles and salad. Serves 6.

Ingredients
For the chicken:
• 1.2 kg boneless skinless chicken thighs
• 60 ml olive oil
• 3 tbsp lemon juice
• 4 garlic cloves, grated
• 2 tbsp plain yogurt
• 2 tsp ground cumin
• 2 tsp ground coriander
• 2 tsp paprika
• 1 tsp ground turmeric
• 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
• 1/2 tsp ground allspice
• 1/4 tsp ground cardamom
• 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
• 1 1/2 tsp salt
• 1/2 tsp black pepper
• 1 red onion, cut into wedges
For the sauce:
• 240 g Greek yogurt
• 2 tbsp tahini
• 1 tbsp lemon juice (for the sauce)
• 1 garlic clove, grated (for the sauce)
• 1/4 tsp salt (for the sauce)
To serve:
• 6 pitas, warmed
• 2 tomatoes, diced
• 1 cucumber, diced
• 120 g pickled turnips or dill pickles
• 15 g parsley, chopped

Steps
1. Mix 60 ml olive oil, 3 tbsp lemon juice, 4 garlic cloves, grated, 2 tbsp plain yogurt, 2 tsp ground cumin, 2 tsp ground coriander, 2 tsp paprika, 1 tsp ground turmeric, 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon, 1/2 tsp ground allspice, 1/4 tsp ground cardamom, 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper, 1 1/2 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp black pepper. Toss with 1.2 kg boneless skinless chicken thighs and refrigerate at least 2 hours, or overnight.
2. Heat the oven to 220°C. Spread the chicken and 1 red onion, cut into wedges on a lined sheet pan in one layer.
3. Roast 25 minutes, until cooked through and charred at the edges. Turn on the broiler and broil 3 minutes for extra char.
4. Whisk 240 g Greek yogurt, 2 tbsp tahini, 1 tbsp lemon juice (for the sauce), 1 garlic clove, grated (for the sauce) and 1/4 tsp salt (for the sauce) with a splash of water until spoonable.
5. Rest the chicken 5 minutes, then slice thinly. For the shawarma-shop texture, crisp the slices in a hot dry skillet for 2 minutes with the pan juices.
6. Fill 6 pitas, warmed with chicken, onions, sauce, 2 tomatoes, diced, 1 cucumber, diced, 120 g pickled turnips or dill pickles and 15 g parsley, chopped.

Notes
The broil and the final skillet crisp mimic the caramelized edges shaved off a vertical spit. Toum (Lebanese whipped garlic sauce) is the traditional pairing if you want to make it; the tahini-yogurt sauce is a quicker stand-in. Also great over rice with salad.
