Baja Fish Tacos — Ensenada-Style Beer-Battered Tacos de Pescado

Ensenada street-cart fish tacos: strips of white fish in a light, crackly beer batter, fried and tucked into warm corn tortillas with shredded cabbage, thin crema, salsa bandera and lime. Serves 4 (12 tacos).

Ingredients
For the fish:
• 680 g firm white fish (cod, halibut, rockfish or mahi-mahi), cut into 2 × 10 cm strips
• 1 tsp salt (for the fish)
• 130 g all-purpose flour
• 1/2 tsp baking powder
• 1 tsp salt (for the batter)
• 1/2 tsp Mexican oregano
• 1 tsp yellow mustard
• 250 ml cold Mexican lager
• 40 g all-purpose flour (for dredging)
• 1 L neutral oil (for frying)
For the crema:
• 120 ml Mexican crema
• 2 tbsp mayonnaise
• 1 tbsp lime juice
• 1 tbsp milk
For the salsa bandera:
• 2 Roma tomatoes, finely diced
• 1/2 white onion, finely diced
• 1 serrano chile, minced
• 1/2 bunch cilantro, chopped
• 1 tbsp lime juice (for the salsa)
• 1/2 tsp salt (for the salsa)
To serve:
• 200 g green cabbage, very finely shredded
• 12 corn tortillas
• 2 limes, cut into wedges

Steps
1. Mix 2 Roma tomatoes, finely diced, 1/2 white onion, finely diced, 1 serrano chile, minced, 1/2 bunch cilantro, chopped, 1 tbsp lime juice (for the salsa) and 1/2 tsp salt (for the salsa) for the salsa. Whisk 120 ml Mexican crema, 2 tbsp mayonnaise, 1 tbsp lime juice and 1 tbsp milk until pourable.
2. Pat 680 g firm white fish (cod, halibut, rockfish or mahi-mahi), cut into 2 × 10 cm strips dry and season with 1 tsp salt (for the fish).
3. Whisk 130 g all-purpose flour, 1/2 tsp baking powder, 1 tsp salt (for the batter) and 1/2 tsp Mexican oregano. Add 1 tsp yellow mustard and 250 ml cold Mexican lager and whisk just until smooth, like thin pancake batter. Don't overmix.
4. Heat 1 L neutral oil (for frying) in a deep pot to 185°C.
5. Dust the fish strips in 40 g all-purpose flour (for dredging), dip in the batter and let the excess drip off, then lower them into the oil away from you. Fry 4 at a time for 4 minutes, turning once, until deep golden and crisp. Drain on a rack.
6. Warm 12 corn tortillas on a hot comal or dry pan for 30 seconds per side, stacking them in a towel.
7. Put a piece of fish in each tortilla (doubled if thin), top with 200 g green cabbage, very finely shredded, a drizzle of crema and salsa, and serve with 2 limes, cut into wedges.

Notes
The batter should be cold and freshly mixed: the beer's bubbles and the baking powder are what make it lacy and light. Let the oil come back to 185°C between batches.

Ensenada stands also set out pickled jalapeños and carrots, guacamole and bottled hot sauce. Shrimp work the same way (tacos de camarón): fry 2 minutes.
