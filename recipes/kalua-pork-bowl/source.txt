Kālua Pork Bowl — Hawaiian Kalua Pig and Cabbage over Rice

Oven kalua pig in the style of the imu (underground oven): pork shoulder rubbed with alaea salt and liquid smoke, wrapped in banana leaves and slow-roasted until it shreds, then cooked down with cabbage and served over rice. Serves 8.

Ingredients
For the kalua pig:
• 2.5 kg bone-in pork shoulder (pork butt)
• 25 g alaea Hawaiian sea salt or coarse sea salt (about 1 1/2 tbsp)
• 2 tbsp liquid smoke (hickory or mesquite)
• 3 large banana leaves or ti leaves (optional)
• 240 ml water
For the bowls:
• 1 small green cabbage (about 900 g), cut into 3 cm pieces
• 1.8 kg hot cooked white rice
• 4 scallions, sliced
• 2 tbsp shoyu, to taste

Steps
1. Heat the oven to 160°C. Pat 2.5 kg bone-in pork shoulder (pork butt) dry and pierce it all over with a knife, 2 cm deep. Rub in 2 tbsp liquid smoke (hickory or mesquite), then 25 g alaea Hawaiian sea salt or coarse sea salt (about 1 1/2 tbsp), working it into the cuts.
2. Wrap the pork in 3 large banana leaves or ti leaves (optional) if using, then wrap tightly in foil. Set it in a roasting pan with 240 ml water and cover the pan with more foil.
3. Roast for 5 hours, until the pork pulls apart easily and the bone slides out.
4. Unwrap, shred the meat with forks, discarding the bone and large pieces of fat, and toss it with the pan juices. Taste: it should be well salted. Let it sit 15 minutes to absorb the juices.
5. Put a third of the shredded pork and 240 ml of the juices in a large skillet over medium-high. Add 1 small green cabbage (about 900 g), cut into 3 cm pieces and cook 8 minutes, stirring, until the cabbage is tender and the liquid has mostly evaporated. Season with 2 tbsp shoyu, to taste.
6. Spoon the kalua pork and cabbage over 1.8 kg hot cooked white rice and top with 4 scallions, sliced.

Notes
Alaea salt is Hawaiian sea salt mixed with red volcanic clay; it's mild and slightly earthy. Plain coarse sea salt works; don't use table salt (it's much saltier by volume). The banana leaves add a subtle vegetal, tea-like aroma.

Slow cooker: rub the same way, cook on low 16 hours with no added water. Instant Pot: cut into 4 pieces, 90 minutes high pressure with 240 ml water. Leftover kalua pig is excellent in fried rice, quesadillas or eggs benedict.
