Jalapeño Grilled Pork Chops

Thick bone-in pork chops marinated in a tangy blend of jalapeño, garlic, lime, cider vinegar, toasted coriander and honey, grilled over high heat and served with a charred jalapeño and scallion salsa. Serves 4.

Ingredients
For the marinade:
• 2 jalapeños, stemmed and roughly chopped
• 4 garlic cloves
• 1 tbsp coriander seeds, toasted
• 60 ml apple cider vinegar
• 2 tbsp lime juice
• 2 tbsp honey
• 60 ml olive oil
• 2 tsp kosher salt
For the pork:
• 4 bone-in pork rib chops, 2.5 cm thick (about 1.2 kg)
For the salsa:
• 4 jalapeños (for charring)
• 6 scallions
• 2 tbsp olive oil (for the salsa)
• 1 tbsp lime juice (for the salsa)
• 10 g cilantro, chopped
• 1/2 tsp salt (for the salsa)

Steps
1. Blend 2 jalapeños, stemmed and roughly chopped, 4 garlic cloves, 1 tbsp coriander seeds, toasted, 60 ml apple cider vinegar, 2 tbsp lime juice, 2 tbsp honey, 60 ml olive oil and 2 tsp kosher salt until smooth.
2. Put 4 bone-in pork rib chops, 2.5 cm thick (about 1.2 kg) in a dish, pour over the marinade and turn to coat. Refrigerate at least 2 hours (up to 8 hours).
3. Heat a grill to medium-high. Grill 4 jalapeños (for charring) and 6 scallions for 6 minutes, turning, until blistered and charred. Chop, removing seeds from the jalapeños for less heat, and mix with 2 tbsp olive oil (for the salsa), 1 tbsp lime juice (for the salsa), 10 g cilantro, chopped and 1/2 tsp salt (for the salsa).
4. Scrape most of the marinade off the chops. Grill 5 minutes per side over direct heat, until charred, then move to the cooler side and cook 4 minutes more, until 60°C near the bone.
5. Rest 5 minutes and serve with the charred salsa.

Notes
Inspired by Bon Appétit's jalapeño-marinated chops. Rib chops with a fat cap stay juicier than center-cut loin. Pull them at 60°C; they rise to a rosy 63°C as they rest. Don't marinate more than 8 hours; the vinegar starts to cure the meat.
