Cacio e Pepe — Pecorino and Black Pepper Pasta

Rome's three-ingredient pasta: toasted cracked pepper and finely grated Pecorino Romano emulsified with starchy pasta water into a creamy sauce, with no butter and no cream. Serves 2.

Ingredients
• 200 g tonnarelli or spaghetti
• 1 tsp salt (for the pasta water)
• 2 tsp black peppercorns
• 100 g Pecorino Romano, very finely grated

Steps
1. Crush 2 tsp black peppercorns coarsely in a mortar. Toast them in a large dry skillet over medium for 1 minute, until fragrant.
2. Bring 1.5 L water to a boil in a wide pot with 1 tsp salt (for the pasta water) (pecorino is salty, so keep it light). Cook 200 g tonnarelli or spaghetti 2 minutes less than the package says. Using less water than usual makes it starchier.
3. Meanwhile, put 100 g Pecorino Romano, very finely grated in a bowl. Ladle a little pasta water into a cup and let it cool 2 minutes, then stir 3–4 tbsp of it into the cheese to make a thick, smooth cream.
4. Add a ladle of pasta water to the pepper in the skillet. Transfer the pasta and toss over medium heat for 2 minutes, adding water as needed, until al dente and coated in a starchy glaze.
5. Take the pan off the heat and wait 30 seconds. Add the cheese cream and toss vigorously for 1 minute, adding pasta water a spoon at a time, until silky. Serve right away.

Notes
Temperature is everything: pecorino seizes into clumps above about 70°C, which is why the water for the cheese cream is cooled and the pan comes off the heat. Grate the cheese on the finest rasp; coarse shreds don't melt smoothly.
