カツカレー (Katsu Karē) · Japanese Curry with Chicken and Spam Katsu

Japanese curry from a homemade roux, simmered with onion, carrot, potato and grated apple, served with rice and two katsu: panko chicken thigh and crispy Spam. Serves 4.

Ingredients
For the curry roux:
• 60 g unsalted butter
• 60 g all-purpose flour (for the roux)
• 2 tbsp Japanese curry powder (S&B)
• 1 tsp garam masala
• 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
For the curry:
• 1 tbsp neutral oil
• 2 onions, thinly sliced
• 1 carrot, cut into chunks
• 2 Yukon Gold potatoes (about 350 g), cut into chunks
• 800 ml chicken stock
• 1/2 apple, grated
• 1 tbsp ketchup
• 1 tbsp tonkatsu sauce or Worcestershire sauce
• 1 tsp soy sauce
• 1 tbsp honey
• 1/2 tsp salt, to taste
For the katsu:
• 2 boneless skinless chicken thighs (about 300 g)
• 1 can Spam (340 g), cut into 4 slices 1.5 cm thick
• 1/2 tsp salt (for the chicken)
• 1/4 tsp black pepper
• 40 g all-purpose flour (for dredging)
• 2 eggs, beaten
• 100 g panko
• 1 L neutral oil (for frying)
To serve:
• 900 g hot cooked short-grain rice
• 4 tbsp fukujinzuke (red pickled relish)

Steps
1. Make the roux: melt 60 g unsalted butter in a small pan over medium-low, add 60 g all-purpose flour (for the roux) and stir constantly for 15 minutes, until the color of peanut butter. Off the heat, stir in 2 tbsp Japanese curry powder (S&B), 1 tsp garam masala and 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper. Set aside.
2. Heat 1 tbsp neutral oil in a heavy pot over medium and cook 2 onions, thinly sliced for 15 minutes, stirring, until soft and golden brown.
3. Add 1 carrot, cut into chunks, 2 Yukon Gold potatoes (about 350 g), cut into chunks and 800 ml chicken stock. Bring to a boil, skim, and simmer covered for 15 minutes, until the potatoes are just tender.
4. Ladle a cup of the hot stock into the roux and whisk smooth, then stir it back into the pot. Add 1/2 apple, grated, 1 tbsp ketchup, 1 tbsp tonkatsu sauce or Worcestershire sauce, 1 tsp soy sauce and 1 tbsp honey. Simmer on low for 10 minutes, stirring often so it doesn't catch, until thick and glossy. Season with 1/2 tsp salt, to taste.
5. Pound 2 boneless skinless chicken thighs (about 300 g) to an even 1.5 cm and season with 1/2 tsp salt (for the chicken) and 1/4 tsp black pepper. Dredge the chicken and 1 can Spam (340 g), cut into 4 slices 1.5 cm thick in 40 g all-purpose flour (for dredging), dip in 2 eggs, beaten, and press firmly into 100 g panko.
6. Heat 1 L neutral oil (for frying) to 170°C. Fry the chicken 6 minutes, turning once, until deep golden and 74°C inside. Fry the Spam 2 minutes, until golden. Drain on a rack and rest the chicken 3 minutes before slicing.
7. Plate 900 g hot cooked short-grain rice on one side, ladle curry on the other, and lay the sliced katsu over the line between them. Serve with 4 tbsp fukujinzuke (red pickled relish).

Notes
Store-bought roux (Golden Curry, Vermont or Java) is how most Japanese homes do it: skip step 1 and stir in 115 g (half a box) at step 4, off the heat. The homemade roux lets you control the spice and avoids the palm oil.

Curry is famously better the next day; reheat gently with a splash of water. Spam is already cooked and salty, so it needs no seasoning, only a hot fry to crisp the panko.
