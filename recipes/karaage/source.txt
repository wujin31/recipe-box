唐揚げ (Karaage) · Japanese Fried Chicken with Kewpie Mayo

Skin-on chicken thighs marinated in soy, sake, ginger and garlic, coated in potato starch and double-fried for a thin, shattering crust and juicy middle. Served with lemon and Kewpie. Serves 4.

Ingredients
• 600 g boneless skin-on chicken thighs, cut into 5 cm pieces
• 2 tbsp soy sauce
• 1 tbsp sake
• 2 tsp grated ginger
• 1 garlic clove, grated
• 1/2 tsp sesame oil
• 1/2 tsp sugar
• 60 g potato starch (katakuriko)
• 1 L neutral oil (for frying)
• 4 tbsp Kewpie mayonnaise
• 1/2 lemon, cut into wedges
• 150 g green cabbage, finely shredded
• shichimi togarashi, to serve

Steps
1. Mix 600 g boneless skin-on chicken thighs, cut into 5 cm pieces with 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp sake, 2 tsp grated ginger, 1 garlic clove, grated, 1/2 tsp sesame oil and 1/2 tsp sugar. Marinate in the fridge for 30 minutes (up to 2 hours).
2. Heat 1 L neutral oil (for frying) to 160°C. Drain the chicken and toss in 60 g potato starch (katakuriko) until each piece is fully coated, squeezing the starch on so the pieces hold a craggy shape.
3. Fry in batches for 3 minutes, until pale and just cooked. Lift onto a rack and rest 4 minutes.
4. Raise the oil to 190°C and fry again for 1 minute, until deep golden and crisp. Drain on the rack.
5. Serve hot with 150 g green cabbage, finely shredded, 1/2 lemon, cut into wedges and 4 tbsp Kewpie mayonnaise, with shichimi togarashi, to serve sprinkled over the mayo if you like.

Notes
Potato starch fries lighter and crisper than flour or cornstarch. The rest between fries lets residual heat finish the inside gently, so the second fry only has to crisp. Skin-on thigh is essential; breast dries out.
