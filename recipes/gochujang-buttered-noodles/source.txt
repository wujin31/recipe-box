Gochujang Buttered Noodles

Eric Kim's much-loved pantry pasta: spaghetti tossed in a glossy sauce of butter, a lot of garlic, gochujang, honey and vinegar, reduced until sticky. 20 minutes. Serves 4.

Ingredients
• 450 g spaghetti (or ramen or any noodle)
• 1 tbsp salt (for the pasta water)
• 6 tbsp unsalted butter
• 12 garlic cloves, finely chopped
• 1/4 tsp salt
• 1/2 tsp black pepper
• 60 g gochujang (1/4 cup)
• 60 ml honey
• 60 ml rice vinegar or sherry vinegar
• 3 scallions, thinly sliced
• 10 g cilantro, chopped

Steps
1. Boil 450 g spaghetti (or ramen or any noodle) in salted water with 1 tbsp salt (for the pasta water) according to the package. Reserve 240 ml pasta water, drain, and return the pasta to the pot.
2. Meanwhile, melt 4 tbsp of 6 tbsp unsalted butter in a skillet over medium-low. Add 12 garlic cloves, finely chopped, 1/4 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp black pepper and cook 3 minutes, until the garlic softens but doesn't brown.
3. Stir in 60 g gochujang (1/4 cup), 60 ml honey and 60 ml rice vinegar or sherry vinegar. Raise the heat to medium-high and simmer 4 minutes, stirring constantly, until reduced to a thick, glossy sauce.
4. Scrape the sauce into the pasta pot with the remaining butter and toss over low heat, adding splashes of pasta water, until the noodles are coated and saucy.
5. Serve topped with 3 scallions, thinly sliced and 10 g cilantro, chopped.

Notes
Adapted from Eric Kim's NYT Cooking recipe. Gochujang brands vary in heat and sweetness; taste the sauce and adjust honey or vinegar. A jammy egg or crispy fried egg on top is excellent.
