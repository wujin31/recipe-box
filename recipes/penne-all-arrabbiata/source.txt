Penne all'Arrabbiata — Penne in Spicy Tomato Sauce

The Roman "angry" pasta: whole peeled tomatoes cooked down quickly with garlic and plenty of dried chili in olive oil, tossed with ridged penne and parsley. Serves 4.

Ingredients
• 400 g penne rigate
• 1 tbsp salt (for the pasta water)
• 60 ml extra-virgin olive oil
• 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
• 3 dried peperoncini, crumbled (or 1 tsp red chili flakes)
• 800 g canned whole peeled tomatoes (San Marzano), crushed by hand
• 1/2 tsp salt (for the sauce)
• 15 g flat-leaf parsley, chopped

Steps
1. Heat 60 ml extra-virgin olive oil with 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced and 3 dried peperoncini, crumbled (or 1 tsp red chili flakes) in a large skillet over medium-low for 3 minutes, until the garlic is pale gold and the oil is red-tinged.
2. Add 800 g canned whole peeled tomatoes (San Marzano), crushed by hand and 1/2 tsp salt (for the sauce), raise the heat to medium and simmer 20 minutes, stirring now and then, until thick and the oil separates at the edges.
3. Meanwhile, bring 4 L water to a boil with 1 tbsp salt (for the pasta water) and cook 400 g penne rigate 1 minute less than the package says.
4. Transfer the pasta to the sauce with a splash of pasta water and toss over high heat for 1 minute, until coated and glossy.
5. Off the heat, toss in 15 g flat-leaf parsley, chopped and serve.

Notes
The heat should be assertive; that's the name. Crushing the tomatoes by hand gives better texture than purée. Romans don't usually add cheese, but a little grated Pecorino Romano is common elsewhere.
