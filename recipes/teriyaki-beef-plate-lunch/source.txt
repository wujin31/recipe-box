Teriyaki Beef Plate Lunch — Hawaiian Plate with Two Scoops Rice and Mac Salad

Local-style teri beef, sweeter and saltier than Japanese teriyaki: thin-sliced beef marinated overnight in shoyu, brown sugar, ginger and pineapple, seared until lacquered, with two scoops rice and creamy Hawaiian macaroni salad. Serves 4.

Ingredients
For the beef:
• 900 g chuck flap, top sirloin or flank steak, sliced 6 mm thick against the grain
• 180 ml shoyu (soy sauce)
• 150 g brown sugar
• 120 ml water
• 60 ml mirin
• 2 tbsp grated ginger
• 4 garlic cloves, grated
• 3 tbsp pineapple juice
• 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
• 1 tsp black pepper
• 1 tbsp neutral oil
• 2 scallions, sliced
• 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds
For the mac salad:
• 225 g elbow macaroni
• 1 tbsp salt (for the pasta water)
• 2 tbsp cider vinegar
• 1 small carrot, finely grated
• 1 tbsp grated onion
• 240 g mayonnaise (Best Foods/Hellmann's)
• 120 ml whole milk
• 1 tsp sugar
• 1/2 tsp black pepper (for the salad)
• 1/2 tsp salt, to taste
To serve:
• 900 g hot cooked white rice

Steps
1. Whisk 180 ml shoyu (soy sauce), 150 g brown sugar, 120 ml water, 60 ml mirin, 2 tbsp grated ginger, 4 garlic cloves, grated, 3 tbsp pineapple juice, 1 tsp toasted sesame oil and 1 tsp black pepper until the sugar dissolves. Set aside 120 ml for the glaze. Add 900 g chuck flap, top sirloin or flank steak, sliced 6 mm thick against the grain to the rest, cover and refrigerate overnight (at least 4 hours).
2. Make the mac salad first, since it needs to chill: boil 225 g elbow macaroni in water with 1 tbsp salt (for the pasta water) for 2 minutes longer than the package says, until soft. Drain, toss with 2 tbsp cider vinegar, 1 small carrot, finely grated and 1 tbsp grated onion, and cool completely.
3. Whisk 240 g mayonnaise (Best Foods/Hellmann's), 120 ml whole milk, 1 tsp sugar and 1/2 tsp black pepper (for the salad), fold into the macaroni, season with 1/2 tsp salt, to taste, and refrigerate 4 hours. It will thicken; stir in a splash of milk before serving.
4. Simmer the reserved marinade in a small pan for 3 minutes, until slightly syrupy.
5. Heat 1 tbsp neutral oil in a cast-iron pan or griddle over high. Shake excess marinade off the beef and sear in batches, 1 minute per side, until caramelized at the edges.
6. Brush the beef with the glaze, scatter with 2 scallions, sliced and 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds, and plate with two scoops of 900 g hot cooked white rice and one scoop of mac salad.

Notes
Mac salad is meant to be soft and creamy, not al dente: overcooking the pasta and dressing it with vinegar while hot lets it absorb the dressing. Plain mayo and milk only; no mustard, relish or celery in the classic plate-lunch version.

Grilling over charcoal is the other traditional way: 2 minutes per side over high heat.
