排骨飯 (Páigǔ Fàn) · Taiwanese Fried Pork Chop Rice

The railway-bento classic: thin pork chops marinated with garlic, five-spice and rice wine, coated in coarse sweet potato starch and fried until craggy and crisp, over rice with garlicky cabbage. Serves 4.

Ingredients
For the pork:
• 4 boneless pork loin chops (about 600 g), 1 cm thick
• 2 tbsp soy sauce
• 1 tbsp Taiwanese rice wine (米酒) or Shaoxing wine
• 1 tsp sugar
• 4 garlic cloves, grated
• 1/2 tsp five-spice powder
• 1/2 tsp ground white pepper
• 1 tsp sesame oil
• 1 egg
• 2 tbsp sweet potato starch (for the marinade)
• 120 g coarse sweet potato starch (地瓜粉), for coating
• 750 ml neutral oil (for frying)
For the pepper salt:
• 1/2 tsp ground white pepper (for the pepper salt)
• 1/4 tsp salt
• 1/8 tsp five-spice powder (for the pepper salt)
To serve:
• 800 g hot cooked short-grain rice
• 400 g green cabbage, torn into pieces
• 2 garlic cloves, sliced (for the cabbage)
• 1 tbsp neutral oil (for the cabbage)
• 1/2 tsp salt (for the cabbage)

Steps
1. Snip the fat and connective tissue around the edge of 4 boneless pork loin chops (about 600 g), 1 cm thick every 2 cm so the chops stay flat. Pound both sides with a mallet or the back of a cleaver to about 8 mm.
2. Mix 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp Taiwanese rice wine (米酒) or Shaoxing wine, 1 tsp sugar, 4 garlic cloves, grated, 1/2 tsp five-spice powder, 1/2 tsp ground white pepper, 1 tsp sesame oil, 1 egg and 2 tbsp sweet potato starch (for the marinade) in a bowl. Add the pork, turn to coat, cover and refrigerate at least 1 hour (or overnight).
3. Put 120 g coarse sweet potato starch (地瓜粉), for coating on a plate. Press each chop firmly into it on both sides so the coarse granules stick. Let the coated chops sit 5 minutes, until the starch looks damp; this keeps the crust from falling off.
4. Heat 750 ml neutral oil (for frying) in a wok or deep pan to 170°C. Fry two chops at a time for 3 minutes, turning once, until pale gold. Lift out onto a rack.
5. Raise the oil to 190°C and fry each chop again for 30 seconds to crisp and darken the crust. Drain on the rack and sprinkle with 1/2 tsp ground white pepper (for the pepper salt), 1/4 tsp salt and 1/8 tsp five-spice powder (for the pepper salt) mixed together.
6. Heat 1 tbsp neutral oil (for the cabbage) in a wok over high, add 2 garlic cloves, sliced (for the cabbage) for 15 seconds, then 400 g green cabbage, torn into pieces and 1/2 tsp salt (for the cabbage). Stir-fry 3 minutes, until just wilted.
7. Cut the chops into strips and serve over 800 g hot cooked short-grain rice with the cabbage.

Notes
Coarse sweet potato starch (the grainy kind in Taiwanese markets, 粗地瓜粉) is what gives the signature crunchy, pebbly crust; cornstarch makes a smooth, thinner one. The 5-minute rest lets the starch absorb marinade (反潮) so it sticks.

For the full bento, spoon some lu rou sauce from your 滷肉飯 over the rice and add a braised egg and pickled mustard greens. Bone-in chops work too; add 1 minute to the first fry.
