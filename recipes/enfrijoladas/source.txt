Enfrijoladas — Tortillas in Black Bean Sauce with Cotija and Crema

The Oaxacan home classic: corn tortillas flashed in hot oil, dipped in a silky black bean sauce with chipotle, folded around crumbled cheese and topped with crema, cotija and raw onion. Serves 4.

Ingredients
For the bean sauce:
• 750 g cooked black beans (or 2 cans, 425 g each), with their liquid
• 250 ml water or bean broth
• 1 tbsp lard or neutral oil
• 1/4 white onion, chopped
• 2 garlic cloves
• 1 chipotle in adobo (or 2 toasted chiles de árbol)
• 2 dried avocado leaves, toasted (optional)
• 1/2 tsp salt, to taste
For the enfrijoladas:
• 60 ml neutral oil (for the tortillas)
• 12 corn tortillas
• 200 g queso fresco, crumbled
To serve:
• 120 ml Mexican crema
• 60 g cotija cheese, finely crumbled
• 1/2 white onion, sliced into thin rings
• 1/4 bunch cilantro, chopped
• 1 avocado, sliced

Steps
1. Heat 1 tbsp lard or neutral oil in a wide skillet over medium. Fry 1/4 white onion, chopped for 4 minutes, until soft, adding 2 garlic cloves for the last 1 minute.
2. Blend the onion and garlic with 750 g cooked black beans (or 2 cans, 425 g each), with their liquid, 250 ml water or bean broth, 1 chipotle in adobo (or 2 toasted chiles de árbol) and 2 dried avocado leaves, toasted (optional) until completely smooth.
3. Pour the purée into the skillet (it will splatter) and simmer 5 minutes, stirring, until it's the thickness of heavy cream. Season with 1/2 tsp salt, to taste. Thin with water if needed; it thickens as it stands. Keep warm over low heat.
4. Heat 60 ml neutral oil (for the tortillas) in another skillet over medium-high. Pass each of 12 corn tortillas through the oil for 5 seconds per side, just to soften and seal, and drain on paper towels.
5. Dip each tortilla in the bean sauce to coat both sides, fold it in half, fill with some of 200 g queso fresco, crumbled, and fold again into quarters. Arrange on plates.
6. Spoon more sauce over the top, then drizzle with 120 ml Mexican crema and scatter with 60 g cotija cheese, finely crumbled, 1/2 white onion, sliced into thin rings, 1/4 bunch cilantro, chopped and 1 avocado, sliced. Serve right away.

Notes
The quick fry seals the tortillas so they don't fall apart in the sauce. Avocado leaves give the Oaxacan anise note; a pinch of ground anise or a sprig of epazote does something similar.

For a fuller meal, fill them with shredded chicken or chorizo and potato, or top with a fried egg for breakfast.
